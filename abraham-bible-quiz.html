1. What was the name of Abraham's wife?

Show answer

A) Sarah (Genesis 17:15). God changed her name from Sarai to Sarah.

2. From what city did Abraham leave, at God's calling?

Show answer

D) Ur of the Chaldeans (Genesis 15:7). The LORD brought Abraham out of Ur of the Chaldeans.

3. What was the name of Abraham and Sarah's promised son?

Show answer

A) Isaac (Genesis 21:3). Sarah bore Abraham a son, and he named him Isaac.

4. What was the name of Abraham's nephew, who lived in Sodom?

Show answer

A) Lot (Genesis 13:11-12). Lot, Abraham's nephew, chose to live toward Sodom.

5. What two cities did God destroy for their sin, in Abraham's time?

Show answer

C) Sodom and Gomorrah (Genesis 19:24-25). The LORD rained down fire and brimstone on Sodom and Gomorrah.

6. What did God command Abram, at his calling, concerning his father's house?

Show answer

A) To leave his country and his father's house (Genesis 12:1). The LORD had said to Abram, "Go from your country, your people and your father's household."

7. What did God promise to make out of Abram, at his calling?

Show answer

C) A great nation (Genesis 12:2). "I will make you into a great nation and I will bless you."

8. Why did Abraham and Lot part ways, each going to a different region?

Show answer

D) A quarrel between their herdsmen, because their flocks were too large (Genesis 13:7-9). A quarrel arose between the herdsmen of Abram's livestock and the herdsmen of Lot's livestock.

9. Where did Abraham's servant first meet Rebekah?

Show answer

B) At a well (Genesis 24:11). The servant made the camels kneel down outside the city, near a well.

10. What sign did Abraham's servant use to recognize the right girl to be Isaac's wife?

Show answer

C) That she would offer him water, and also water for his camels (Genesis 24:14). "The girl to whom I say, 'Please let down your jar that I may drink,'... that will be the sign that she is the one appointed for your servant Isaac."

11. What did God establish as a sign of the covenant with Abraham, commanded for every man in his family?

Show answer

B) Circumcision (Genesis 17:10). "This is my covenant... Every male among you shall be circumcised."

12. Who was the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother, and Rebekah's grandmother?

Show answer

B) Milcah (Genesis 22:20-23). Nahor married Milcah, daughter of his brother Haran; she became Rebekah's grandmother, through their son Bethuel.

13. At what city did the family of Terah, Abraham's father, stop, on the way to Canaan?

Show answer

C) Haran (Genesis 11:31). They came to Haran and settled there.

14. What was the name of Sarah's maidservant, Ishmael's mother?

Show answer

B) Hagar (Genesis 16:15). Hagar, the Egyptian, bore Abraham a son, Ishmael.

15. On what mountain did God ask Abraham to offer Isaac as a sacrifice?

Show answer

B) Mount Moriah (Genesis 22:2). God sent Abraham to the region of Moriah, to a mountain He would show him.

16. What did God provide in Isaac's place, as a sacrifice?

Show answer

C) A ram (Genesis 22:13). Abraham saw a ram caught by its horns in a thicket and offered it as a burnt offering.

17. What did Lot's wife turn into when she looked back at Sodom?

Show answer

B) A pillar of salt (Genesis 19:26). Because she looked back, she became a pillar of salt.

18. How many men (angels) visited Abraham's tent at the oaks of Mamre?

Show answer

B) Three (Genesis 18:2). Abraham looked up and saw three men standing near him.

19. What happened to Sarai in Egypt, when Abram, out of fear, said she was his sister?

Show answer

A) She was taken into Pharaoh's house, who was then struck with great plagues (Genesis 12:14-17). Pharaoh praised Sarai to him... and took her into his house. But the LORD struck Pharaoh with great plagues.

20. Who attacked the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah, taking Lot captive, before Abraham rescued him?

Show answer

D) Chedorlaomer and the kings allied with him (Genesis 14:9-12). Chedorlaomer and the kings with him defeated them... they carried off Lot, Abram's nephew, and his possessions.

21. Who was the priest-king of Salem who met Abraham with bread and wine, after his victory over the kings?

Show answer

D) Melchizedek (Genesis 14:18). Melchizedek king of Salem brought out bread and wine. He was priest of God Most High.

22. How much did Abraham give Melchizedek, out of all the plunder of war?

Show answer

B) A tithe (a tenth) (Genesis 14:20). Abraham gave him a tenth of everything.

23. How did Sarah react when she heard that, in her old age, she would have a son?

Show answer

A) She laughed to herself, in disbelief (Genesis 18:12). Sarah laughed to herself, saying, "After I am worn out and my lord is old, will I now have this pleasure?"

24. How many years did God foretell that Abraham's descendants would be enslaved in a foreign land?

Show answer

A) 400 years (Genesis 15:13). "Know for certain that your descendants will be strangers in a country not their own... four hundred years."

25. What land did Lot choose for his flocks, when he parted from Abraham?

Show answer

D) The plain of the Jordan, toward Sodom (Genesis 13:10-12). Lot chose for himself the whole plain of the Jordan... and pitched his tents near Sodom.

26. How old was Abraham when he himself was circumcised, as a sign of the covenant?

Show answer

C) 99 years old (Genesis 17:24). Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised.
