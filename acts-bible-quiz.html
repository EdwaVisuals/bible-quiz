1. What event happened on the Day of Pentecost, with a strong wind and tongues of fire?

Show answer

C) The descent of the Holy Spirit on the apostles (Acts 2:1-4). A sound like the blowing of a violent wind came from heaven... they saw what seemed to be tongues of fire that separated and came to rest on each of them.

2. How many people were baptized, according to Acts, after Peter's Pentecost sermon?

Show answer

C) About 3,000 (Acts 2:41). Those who accepted his message were baptized, and about three thousand were added to their number that day.

3. Who was the first Christian martyr, stoned to death?

Show answer

A) Stephen (Acts 7:59-60). They dragged him out of the city and began to stone him... While they were stoning him, Stephen prayed, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

4. Who healed a lame man at the Temple gate, called the Beautiful Gate?

Show answer

A) Peter and John (Acts 3:6-8). Peter said, "In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, walk!"

5. Who was the persecutor of Christians, who later became an apostle, on the road to Damascus?

Show answer

C) Saul (the future Paul) (Acts 9:1-6). Saul was still breathing out murderous threats against the Lord's disciples... suddenly a light from heaven flashed around him.

6. Who was chosen by casting lots to replace Judas, among the twelve apostles?

Show answer

C) Matthias (Acts 1:26). Then they cast lots, and the lot fell to Matthias; so he was added to the eleven apostles.

7. What did Barnabas sell, bringing the money to the apostles' feet, for the church's benefit?

Show answer

C) A field (Acts 4:36-37). Joseph, whom the apostles called Barnabas... sold a field he owned and brought the money and put it at the apostles' feet.

8. Who was the sorcerer from Samaria who wanted to buy from the apostles the power to give the Holy Spirit?

Show answer

C) Simon (Acts 8:9,18-19). Simon... offered them money and said, "Give me also this ability."

9. How many days did Saul remain blind, after his vision on the road to Damascus, without eating or drinking?

Show answer

B) Three days (Acts 9:9). For three days he was blind, and did not eat or drink anything.

10. Who was sent by the Lord to restore Saul's sight, in Damascus?

Show answer

B) Ananias (Acts 9:10-11). The Lord told Ananias, "Go to the house of Judas on Straight Street and ask for a man from Tarsus named Saul."

11. What fell from Saul's eyes, when Ananias placed his hands on him, in Damascus?

Show answer

A) Something like scales (Acts 9:18). Immediately, something like scales fell from Saul's eyes, and he could see again.

12. Whom did Peter raise from the dead, at Joppa, a woman known for her acts of charity?

Show answer

C) Tabitha (Dorcas) (Acts 9:40). Peter... got down on his knees and prayed. Then he said, "Tabitha, get up." She opened her eyes.

13. What were the disciples doing together, in the upper room, right before Pentecost?

Show answer

D) They joined together constantly in prayer (Acts 1:14). They all joined together constantly in prayer, along with the women.

14. Who preached the Gospel in Samaria, bringing great joy to the city?

Show answer

A) Philip (Acts 8:5,8). Philip went down to a city in Samaria and proclaimed the Messiah there... there was great joy in that city.

15. What did an angel command Philip, directing him to the meeting with the Ethiopian eunuch?

Show answer

A) To go south, to the road that goes down to Gaza (Acts 8:26). An angel of the Lord said to Philip, "Go south to the road — the desert road — that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza."

16. Which couple lied about the money given to the church, dying for their deception?

Show answer

A) Ananias and Sapphira (Acts 5:1-10). When Ananias heard this, he fell down and died... The same thing happened to his wife Sapphira.

17. How many men were chosen as the first deacons, to serve at tables, among them Stephen?

Show answer

A) 7 (Acts 6:3-5). They chose Stephen... and six others, seven men in all, to wait on tables.

18. Who was baptized by Philip, on the road to Gaza, an important official of the Ethiopian queen?

Show answer

D) The Ethiopian eunuch (Acts 8:27-38). Philip told him the good news about Jesus, and the eunuch believed and was baptized.

19. What did Peter see in a vision, which prepared him to preach to the Gentiles?

Show answer

B) A large sheet with all kinds of animals, let down from heaven (Acts 10:9-16). He saw heaven opened and something like a large sheet being let down to earth by its four corners, containing all kinds of animals.

20. Who was the Roman centurion, the first Gentile baptized, through Peter's ministry?

Show answer

B) Cornelius (Acts 10:1,44-48). Cornelius, a centurion, a devout and God-fearing man... was baptized, he and his whole household.

21. What prophecy did Peter quote, at Pentecost, about the outpouring of the Spirit on all people?

Show answer

C) The prophecy of Joel (Acts 2:16-17). This is what was spoken by the prophet Joel: "In the last days... I will pour out my Spirit on all people."

22. How did the members of the Sanhedrin view the boldness of Peter and John, though they were "unschooled, ordinary men"?

Show answer

C) They were astonished, recognizing they had been with Jesus (Acts 4:13). When they saw the courage of Peter and John and realized that they were unschooled, ordinary men, they were astonished and took note that these men had been with Jesus.

23. What happened to the place where the believers were praying, right after Peter and John's release?

Show answer

D) It shook (Acts 4:31). After they prayed, the place where they were meeting was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit.

24. What advice did Gamaliel give the Sanhedrin, concerning the apostles, so as not to oppose God's work?

Show answer

B) "If it is from God, you will not be able to stop it" (Acts 5:38-39). If it is of human origin, it will fail. But if it is from God, you will not be able to stop these men.

25. What did Stephen's face look like, during his trial before the Sanhedrin?

Show answer

A) Like the face of an angel (Acts 6:15). All who were sitting in the Sanhedrin looked intently at Stephen, and they saw that his face was like the face of an angel.

26. What did Stephen see, just before his death, looking intently up to heaven?

Show answer

D) Heaven open, with Jesus standing at the right hand of God (Acts 7:55). Stephen... looked up to heaven and saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God.

27. How did Saul escape Damascus, when the Jews wanted to kill him, watching the city gates?

Show answer

D) He was lowered in a basket, over the wall (Acts 9:25). His followers took him by night and lowered him in a basket through an opening in the wall.

28. What did Peter answer the amazed crowd, at Pentecost, when they asked, "What shall we do?"

Show answer

C) "Repent and be baptized, every one of you" (Acts 2:37-38). Peter replied, "Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, for the forgiveness of your sins."

29. What does the name Barnabas mean, given to Joseph by the apostles, according to the explanation in Acts?

Show answer

A) "Son of encouragement" (Acts 4:36). Joseph, whom the apostles called Barnabas (which means "son of encouragement")...
