1. On what day of creation was light made, according to Genesis?

Show answer

D) The first day (Genesis 1:3-5). God said, "Let there be light," and there was light, on the first day of creation.

2. Who was the first woman, created from Adam's rib?

Show answer

D) Eve (Genesis 3:20). Eve was called "the mother of all living."

3. What did God promise to make out of Abram, at his calling?

Show answer

D) A great nation (Genesis 12:2). "I will make you into a great nation and I will bless you."

4. What did God establish as a sign of the covenant with Abraham, commanded for every man in his family?

Show answer

B) Circumcision (Genesis 17:10). "This is my covenant... Every male among you shall be circumcised."

5. Who was Joseph's father?

Show answer

D) Jacob (Genesis 37:2-3). Joseph was the son of Jacob, born to Rachel.

6. What special garment did Jacob give Joseph, which stirred his brothers' envy?

Show answer

B) A richly ornamented coat of many colors (Genesis 37:3). Israel made Joseph a richly ornamented coat, a sign that he loved him more than his other sons.

7. What animals overran Egypt, in the second plague?

Show answer

A) Frogs (Exodus 8:6). Frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

8. What was baby Moses placed in, to float on the waters of the Nile?

Show answer

C) A basket of papyrus reeds (Exodus 2:3). His mother took a papyrus basket, coated it with tar and pitch, and placed it among the reeds along the bank.

9. What did the spies bring back from Canaan, as proof of the land's fruitfulness?

Show answer

B) A huge cluster of grapes (Numbers 13:23). They cut off a branch with a single cluster of grapes and carried it on a pole between two of them.

10. With what were the assemblies of the people and the breaking of camp announced, through the wilderness?

Show answer

D) Silver trumpets (Numbers 10:2). "Make two trumpets of hammered silver... they are to be used for calling the community together."

11. Who succeeded Moses as leader of the people of Israel?

Show answer

C) Joshua (Joshua 1:1-2). The LORD commanded Joshua son of Nun to lead the people after Moses' death.

12. Who appeared to Joshua near Jericho, with a drawn sword in his hand?

Show answer

C) The commander of the LORD's army (Joshua 5:13). Joshua looked up and saw a man standing in front of him with a drawn sword in his hand.

13. Who killed Sisera, the enemy general, by driving a tent peg through his temple?

Show answer

B) Jael (Judges 4:21). Jael welcomed Sisera into her tent, and while he slept, she drove a tent peg through his temple.

14. What was the period called in which Israel was led by temporary deliverers, before the kings?

Show answer

A) The period of the Judges (Judges 2:16). After Joshua's death, the LORD raised up judges from time to time, to deliver the people from their oppressors.

15. Whom did Samuel serve, as a boy, in the LORD's Tent at Shiloh?

Show answer

A) The priest Eli (1 Samuel 1:24-28). Hannah brought young Samuel to the priest Eli, so he could serve the LORD there for his whole life.

16. What garment did Hannah sew, every year, for her son Samuel, who served at Shiloh?

Show answer

D) A little robe (1 Samuel 2:19). Each year his mother made him a little robe and took it to him when she went up with her husband to offer the sacrifice.

17. How tall was the giant Goliath, according to Scripture?

Show answer

A) Six cubits and a span (1 Samuel 17:4). His height was six cubits and a span.

18. What was David's occupation before he became king?

Show answer

A) Shepherd (1 Samuel 16:11). David tended his father Jesse's sheep in Bethlehem, before being anointed king.

19. What queen came from afar to visit Solomon, amazed by his wisdom and wealth?

Show answer

D) The Queen of Sheba (1 Kings 10:1). The Queen of Sheba heard of Solomon's fame and came to test him with hard questions.

20. Who was King Solomon's father?

Show answer

C) David (2 Samuel 12:24). Solomon was the son of David and Bathsheba, their second child.

21. Where did Elijah flee, a forty-day journey, out of fear of Jezebel, after the contest on Carmel?

Show answer

D) Mount Horeb (1 Kings 19:8). He got up and ate and drank... and traveled forty days and forty nights until he reached Horeb, the mountain of God.

22. Which widow had flour and oil that never ran out, because of Elijah's miracle?

Show answer

A) The widow of Zarephath (1 Kings 17:14). Elijah said, "The jar of flour will not be used up and the jug of oil will not run dry, until the day the LORD sends rain."

23. Who was thrown into the lions' den, for praying to God despite the king's decree?

Show answer

B) Daniel (Daniel 6:16). Daniel was thrown into the lions' den because he kept praying to his God three times a day, defying the king's decree.

24. Which queen was removed, for refusing to come at the command of King Ahasuerus, making way for Esther?

Show answer

A) Vashti (Esther 1:12). Queen Vashti refused to come at the king's command... the king became furious.

25. Who led the first group of Jews returning from exile, as governor, laying the Temple's foundation?

Show answer

C) Zerubbabel (Ezra 3:8-10). Zerubbabel and those who returned with him laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD.

26. What position did Nehemiah hold at the Persian king's court, before going to Jerusalem?

Show answer

D) The king's cupbearer (Nehemiah 1:11; 2:1). Nehemiah was cupbearer to King Artaxerxes, a position of great trust at court.

27. What did Mary do with everything that happened at Jesus's birth?

Show answer

D) She treasured them in her heart and pondered them (Luke 2:19). Mary treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart.

28. What did the shepherds say to one another, urging each other to go to Bethlehem, after the angels departed?

Show answer

A) "Let's go to Bethlehem and see what has happened" (Luke 2:15). The shepherds said to one another, "Let's go to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has told us about."

29. What did Jesus use to heal a man born blind?

Show answer

B) He spit on the ground, made mud, and put it on the man's eyes (John 9:6). He spit on the ground, made some mud with the saliva, and put it on the man's eyes.

30. Who baptized Jesus in the Jordan River?

Show answer

D) John the Baptist (Matthew 3:13-17). Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan, to John, to be baptized by him.

31. Who cut off the ear of the high priest's servant, during Jesus's arrest?

Show answer

B) Peter (Matthew 26:51). One of Jesus's companions... drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.

32. What did Jesus cry out in a loud voice, just before dying, showing the suffering of separation?

Show answer

B) "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (Matthew 27:46). About the ninth hour Jesus cried out in a loud voice, "Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?" (which means "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?").

33. What did Barnabas sell, bringing the money to the apostles' feet, for the church's benefit?

Show answer

B) A field (Acts 4:36-37). Joseph, whom the apostles called Barnabas... sold a field he owned and brought the money and put it at the apostles' feet.

34. What event happened on the Day of Pentecost, with a strong wind and tongues of fire?

Show answer

A) The descent of the Holy Spirit on the apostles (Acts 2:1-4). A sound like the blowing of a violent wind came from heaven... they saw what seemed to be tongues of fire that separated and came to rest on each of them.

35. In what city was Paul imprisoned together with Silas, freed by an earthquake?

Show answer

D) Philippi (Acts 16:23-26). Suddenly there was such a violent earthquake that the foundations of the prison were shaken, and all the doors flew open.

36. What was Paul's name before his conversion to Christianity?

Show answer

C) Saul (Acts 13:9). Saul, who was also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, began to preach.

37. What does Paul write about salvation, through grace and faith, in the epistle to the Ephesians?

Show answer

B) "By grace you have been saved, through faith... not by works" (Ephesians 2:8-9). For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God — not by works.

38. Who was exiled to the island of Patmos, where he received the visions of Revelation?

Show answer

D) The apostle John (Revelation 1:9). I, John... was on the island of Patmos because of the word of God.

39. What new city comes down from heaven, at the end of Revelation, as the eternal dwelling of God with people?

Show answer

C) The New Jerusalem (Revelation 21:2). I saw the Holy City, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God.

40. How was the hair of "one like a son of man" described, in John's first vision?

Show answer

C) White like wool, as white as snow (Revelation 1:14). The hair on his head was white like wool, as white as snow.
