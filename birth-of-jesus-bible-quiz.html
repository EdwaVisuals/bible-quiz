1. What was the name of the angel who announced to Mary that she would give birth to the Messiah?

Show answer

D) Gabriel (Luke 1:26-31). The angel Gabriel was sent by God to Mary, to announce the birth of Jesus.

2. In what city was Jesus born?

Show answer

C) Bethlehem (Luke 2:4-7). Joseph went up from Nazareth to Bethlehem, the town of David, where Jesus was born.

3. Who were the first to hear the news of Jesus's birth, from angels, in the field?

Show answer

C) The shepherds (Luke 2:8-15). An angel of the Lord announced to the shepherds keeping watch over their flocks in the field: "A Savior has been born to you."

4. How many gifts did the magi (wise men) bring to the infant Jesus?

Show answer

B) 3: gold, frankincense and myrrh (Matthew 2:11). The magi presented gifts: gold, frankincense and myrrh.

5. What was the name of Mary's husband, Jesus's adoptive father?

Show answer

B) Joseph (Matthew 1:19-24). Joseph took Mary home as his wife, as the angel of the Lord had commanded him.

6. What did the angel Gabriel say to Mary, to calm her at the angelic news?

Show answer

B) "Do not be afraid, Mary, you have found favor with God" (Luke 1:30). The angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary; you have found favor with God."

7. What did Mary reply to the angel, wondering how this birth would happen, being a virgin?

Show answer

A) "How will this be, since I am a virgin?" (Luke 1:34). Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I am a virgin?"

8. What did Mary reply, submitting to God's will, after the angel's explanation?

Show answer

C) "I am the Lord's servant; may your word to me be fulfilled" (Luke 1:38). "I am the Lord's servant," Mary answered. "May your word to me be fulfilled." Then the angel left her.

9. What did the child in Elizabeth's womb do, at Mary's greeting, the visitor?

Show answer

C) It leaped for joy (Luke 1:41). As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leaped in her womb.

10. What song of praise did the angels sing, at the birth of Jesus, heard by the shepherds?

Show answer

A) "Glory to God in the highest" (Luke 2:14). "Glory to God in the highest heaven, and on earth peace to those on whom his favor rests."

11. What did Mary do with everything that happened at Jesus's birth?

Show answer

B) She treasured them in her heart and pondered them (Luke 2:19). Mary treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart.

12. What was the name of the elderly prophetess who recognized Jesus as the Messiah, at the Temple, alongside Simeon?

Show answer

D) Anna (Luke 2:36-38). There was also a prophet, Anna... coming up to them at that very moment, she began to give thanks to God.

13. What did the angel first say to the shepherds, so they would not be afraid at his appearance?

Show answer

A) "Do not be afraid, I bring you good news" (Luke 2:10). The angel said to them, "Do not be afraid. I bring you good news that will cause great joy for all the people."

14. What did the shepherds say to one another, urging each other to go to Bethlehem, after the angels departed?

Show answer

D) "Let's go to Bethlehem and see what has happened" (Luke 2:15). The shepherds said to one another, "Let's go to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has told us about."

15. What heavenly sign did the magi from the east follow, to the place where the child was?

Show answer

C) A star (Matthew 2:2,9-10). The star they had seen when it rose went ahead of them until it stopped over the place where the child was.

16. Which king ordered the killing of all boys in Bethlehem under two years old, fearing a new king?

Show answer

C) Herod (Matthew 2:16). Herod gave orders to kill all the boys in Bethlehem and its vicinity who were two years old and under.

17. Where did Jesus's family flee, to escape Herod's wrath?

Show answer

B) To Egypt (Matthew 2:13-14). Joseph got up, took the child and his mother during the night and left for Egypt.

18. Who was Mary's relative, John the Baptist's mother, whom Mary visited?

Show answer

B) Elizabeth (Luke 1:39-41). Mary went to the hill country, to a town in Judah, entered Zechariah's home and greeted Elizabeth.

19. Why did Joseph and Mary have to go to Bethlehem, right before Jesus's birth?

Show answer

B) A census ordered by Caesar Augustus (Luke 2:1-5). Caesar Augustus issued a decree that a census should be taken of the entire empire, and everyone had to go to their own town.

20. Where was Zechariah, John the Baptist's father, when the angel Gabriel appeared to announce his son's birth?

Show answer

A) In the Temple, burning incense (Luke 1:8-11). While Zechariah was serving as priest before God... an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense.

21. What name did Zechariah write on a tablet, regaining his speech, to the amazement of all the relatives?

Show answer

A) "His name is John" (Luke 1:63). He asked for a writing tablet, and to everyone's astonishment he wrote, "His name is John."

22. What song of praise did Zechariah utter, as soon as his tongue was loosed, at John's birth?

Show answer

C) Zechariah's Song (Benedictus) (Luke 1:67-68). His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied: "Praise be to the Lord, the God of Israel."

23. What question did the magi ask, arriving in Jerusalem, which troubled Herod?

Show answer

B) "Where is the one who has been born king of the Jews?" (Matthew 2:2). "Where is the one who has been born king of the Jews? We saw his star when it rose and have come to worship him."

24. What did Herod secretly ask the magi, under the pretext that he too wanted to worship the child?

Show answer

D) To return and report back to him where the child was (Matthew 2:7-8). Herod... sent them to Bethlehem and said, "Go and search carefully for the child... report to me."

25. How were the magi warned not to return to Herod, after finding the child?

Show answer

B) Through a dream (Matthew 2:12). Having been warned in a dream not to go back to Herod, they returned to their country by another route.

26. What did the angel tell Joseph, in Egypt, as a sign that they could safely return?

Show answer

B) That Herod had died (Matthew 2:19-20). An angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt and said, "Get up... for those who were trying to take the child's life are dead."

27. What did the magi do, as soon as they found the child in the house, before presenting their gifts?

Show answer

C) They bowed down and worshiped him (Matthew 2:11). On coming to the house, they saw the child with his mother Mary, and they bowed down and worshiped him.
