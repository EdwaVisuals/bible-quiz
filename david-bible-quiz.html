1. Who killed the Philistine giant Goliath, with a sling and a stone?

Show answer

C) David (1 Samuel 17:49-50). Young David struck down Goliath with a single stone, thrown from his sling.

2. What was David's occupation before he became king?

Show answer

C) Shepherd (1 Samuel 16:11). David tended his father Jesse's sheep in Bethlehem, before being anointed king.

3. Who was David's closest friend, the son of King Saul?

Show answer

A) Jonathan (1 Samuel 18:1,3). Jonathan, Saul's son, made a covenant with David, loving him "as himself."

4. What city did David conquer and make the capital of his united kingdom?

Show answer

A) Jerusalem (2 Samuel 5:6-9). David conquered the city of the Jebusites and named it the "City of David" — Jerusalem.

5. How many years did David reign over Israel, in total?

Show answer

B) 40 years (2 Samuel 5:4-5). David reigned forty years — seven at Hebron over Judah, and thirty-three at Jerusalem over all Israel.

6. How many smooth stones did David take from the stream, before facing Goliath?

Show answer

A) Five (1 Samuel 17:40). He chose five smooth stones from the stream and put them in his shepherd's bag.

7. How tall was the giant Goliath, according to Scripture?

Show answer

D) Six cubits and a span (1 Samuel 17:4). His height was six cubits and a span.

8. With whose sword did David cut off Goliath's head, after striking him down with the sling?

Show answer

C) With Goliath's own sword (1 Samuel 17:51). David ran and stood over him; he took hold of the Philistine's sword and drew it from the sheath... and cut off his head with it.

9. What was the name of Saul's daughter, given to David as a wife?

Show answer

B) Michal (1 Samuel 18:20,27). Michal, Saul's daughter, was in love with David; she was given to him as a wife.

10. What unusual bride price did Saul demand from David, for Michal's hand, hoping David would die?

Show answer

C) A hundred Philistine foreskins (1 Samuel 18:25). Saul said, "Say to David, 'The king wants no other price for the bride than a hundred Philistine foreskins.'"

11. What did Michal put in David's bed, to help him escape through the window from the men Saul sent to kill him?

Show answer

C) An idol (teraphim), covered with a garment (1 Samuel 19:13-14). Michal took an idol and laid it on the bed... she told Saul's men that David was ill.

12. Who was the son of Jonathan, lame in both feet, to whom David showed kindness, for the love of the covenant?

Show answer

C) Mephibosheth (2 Samuel 9:1,6). David asked, "Is there anyone still left of the house of Saul to whom I can show kindness for Jonathan's sake?"

13. Who mocked David, despising him as too young, just before the battle with Goliath?

Show answer

A) Eliab, his older brother (1 Samuel 17:28). Eliab burned with anger at David and asked, "Why have you come down here?... I know how conceited you are and how wicked your heart is."

14. How many of Jesse's older sons did Samuel see, before choosing David, the youngest, as the LORD's anointed?

Show answer

D) 7 older brothers (1 Samuel 16:10-13). Samuel saw seven of Jesse's sons, but the LORD had not chosen any of them; only then did they bring David, the youngest, in from the sheep.

15. How many times did David spare Saul's life, though he could have killed him, while fleeing from him?

Show answer

B) Twice (1 Samuel 24:4-7; 26:7-12). David spared Saul twice — once in a cave at En Gedi, cutting off only a corner of his robe, and once in the camp at Hachilah.

16. What did David do with Bathsheba, the wife of Uriah the Hittite, a sin for which the prophet Nathan rebuked him sharply?

Show answer

D) He committed adultery with her, then had Uriah killed (2 Samuel 11:2-17). David committed adultery with Bathsheba, then, to cover his sin, arranged for Uriah, her husband, to die in battle.

17. What parable did the prophet Nathan tell David, to make him recognize his sin?

Show answer

C) The parable of the poor man's ewe lamb (2 Samuel 12:1-7). Nathan told David about a rich man who took a poor man's only lamb, stirring David's anger — before saying, "You are that man!"

18. Who was the son of David who rebelled against him, trying to seize the throne?

Show answer

B) Absalom (2 Samuel 15:1-14). Absalom, David's son, plotted a revolt and drove his father out of Jerusalem for a time.

19. What did David eat from the priest Ahimelek, at Nob, while a fugitive and hungry?

Show answer

D) The consecrated bread (1 Samuel 21:4-6). The priest gave him the consecrated bread, since there was no bread there except the bread of the Presence.

20. Before which Philistine king did David feign madness, to escape with his life?

Show answer

B) Achish, king of Gath (1 Samuel 21:12-13). David... pretended to be insane; he made marks on the doors of the gate and let saliva run down his beard.

21. Where did 400 men in distress gather to David, becoming the core of his army?

Show answer

B) The cave of Adullam (1 Samuel 22:1-2). All those who were in distress or in debt or discontented gathered around him, about four hundred men.

22. Who was made king over the rest of Israel, while David reigned only over Judah, at Hebron?

Show answer

B) Ish-Bosheth, Saul's son (2 Samuel 2:8-9). Abner, the commander of Saul's army, took Ish-Bosheth son of Saul and made him king over Israel.

23. Who was struck dead suddenly by the LORD for touching the ark, while it was being brought toward Jerusalem?

Show answer

D) Uzzah (2 Samuel 6:6-7). Uzzah reached out and took hold of the ark of God, because the oxen stumbled. The LORD's anger burned against Uzzah, and he struck him down.

24. What did David do before the ark, at its triumphant entry into Jerusalem, to Michal's contempt?

Show answer

C) He danced with all his might (2 Samuel 6:14-16). David was dancing before the LORD with all his might... Michal daughter of Saul watched from a window and despised him in her heart.

25. What did three of David's mighty men do, risking their lives, to fulfill a wish of his?

Show answer

A) They broke through the Philistine camp to bring him water from the well of Bethlehem (2 Samuel 23:15-16). The three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp and drew water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem.

26. What did Saul promise the people, besides his daughter's hand, to whoever would defeat Goliath?

Show answer

A) Exemption from taxes for his father's family (1 Samuel 17:25). The king will give great wealth to the man who kills him. He will also give him his daughter in marriage and will exempt his family from taxes in Israel.
