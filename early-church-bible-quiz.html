1. Who wrote "Love is patient, love is kind," in the famous "love chapter"?

Show answer

C) The apostle Paul (1 Corinthians 13:4). Love is patient, love is kind... Paul writes to the Corinthians.

2. What does the apostle James say about faith without deeds?

Show answer

D) That it is dead (James 2:17,26). Faith by itself, if it is not accompanied by action, is dead.

3. Who was exiled to the island of Patmos, where he received the visions of Revelation?

Show answer

D) The apostle John (Revelation 1:9). I, John... was on the island of Patmos because of the word of God.

4. What does Jesus command his disciples to do for one another, as a "new command"?

Show answer

C) To love one another (John 13:34). A new command I give you: Love one another.

5. How many fruits of the Spirit does the apostle Paul list in the epistle to the Galatians?

Show answer

B) 9 (Galatians 5:22-23). The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control.

6. What does Paul write about all people, concerning sin, in the epistle to the Romans?

Show answer

A) That all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God (Romans 3:23). For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.

7. What does Paul write about the wages of sin, compared to the gift of God?

Show answer

C) "The wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life" (Romans 6:23). For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

8. What did Paul write about his strength in Christ, in the epistle to the Philippians?

Show answer

B) "I can do all this through him who gives me strength" (Philippians 4:13). I can do all this through him who gives me strength.

9. What does Paul write about salvation, through grace and faith, in the epistle to the Ephesians?

Show answer

A) "By grace you have been saved, through faith... not by works" (Ephesians 2:8-9). For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God — not by works.

10. What does James say about anyone who lacks wisdom?

Show answer

C) To ask God, who gives generously to all (James 1:5). If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault.

11. What does the apostle John write about confessing sins?

Show answer

C) If we confess our sins, God is faithful and just to forgive us (1 John 1:9). If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins.

12. To what animal does Peter compare the devil, warning believers to be alert?

Show answer

B) A roaring lion, looking for someone to devour (1 Peter 5:8). Your enemy the devil prowls around like a roaring lion looking for someone to devour.

13. What does Paul write about things being permissible, but not all beneficial, in 1 Corinthians?

Show answer

D) "I have the right to do anything, but not everything is beneficial" (1 Corinthians 6:12). "I have the right to do anything," you say — but not everything is beneficial.

14. What image does Paul use to describe the unity of the church, with different members but one whole?

Show answer

A) A body, with many parts (1 Corinthians 12:12). Just as a body, though one, has many parts... so it is with Christ.

15. What spiritual armor does Paul describe in the epistle to the Ephesians, for the Christian's struggle?

Show answer

A) The armor of God (Ephesians 6:11-17). Put on the full armor of God... the breastplate of righteousness, the shield of faith, the helmet of salvation.

16. Who wrote the epistles addressed to persecuted Christians, encouraging them to stand firm in suffering?

Show answer

B) The apostle Peter (1 Peter 1:6-7). In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while you may have had to suffer grief in all kinds of trials, Peter writes.

17. What conditions does Paul set for those who wish to serve as overseers (bishops) in the church?

Show answer

B) To be above reproach, faithful to their spouse, among other things (1 Timothy 3:1-7). Now the overseer is to be above reproach, faithful to his wife, temperate, self-controlled, respectable.

18. What command does Jesus give his disciples in Acts 1:8, about spreading the Gospel?

Show answer

A) To be witnesses in Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, and to the ends of the earth (Acts 1:8). You will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.

19. What does Paul write about all things working together, for those who love God?

Show answer

D) "In all things God works for the good of those who love him" (Romans 8:28). And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him.

20. What does Paul ask the believers in Colossae, concerning how they do their work?

Show answer

A) Whatever they do, to work at it with all their heart, as for the Lord (Colossians 3:23). Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters.

21. What does Paul write to Timothy about the origin and usefulness of Scripture?

Show answer

D) All Scripture is God-breathed and useful (2 Timothy 3:16). All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training.

22. How does the epistle to the Hebrews define faith, at the beginning of chapter 11?

Show answer

B) "Confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see" (Hebrews 11:1). Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see.

23. What does James say about trials, urging believers to an unexpected attitude?

Show answer

B) To consider them "pure joy," for they produce perseverance (James 1:2-3). Consider it pure joy, my brothers and sisters, whenever you face trials of many kinds, because you know that the testing of your faith produces perseverance.

24. What advice does James give about the right attitude in speech and anger?

Show answer

B) To be quick to listen, slow to speak, slow to become angry (James 1:19). Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak and slow to become angry.

25. To what did Paul compare the life of faith, in 1 Corinthians, speaking of an imperishable crown?

Show answer

A) A runner in a race (1 Corinthians 9:24-25). Do you not know that in a race all the runners run, but only one gets the prize? ... They do it to get a crown that will not last.

26. What virtue does the apostle John praise in Gaius, in his third epistle?

Show answer

D) His hospitality toward the brothers, even toward strangers (3 John 1:5). Dear friend, you are faithful in what you are doing for the brothers and sisters, even though they are strangers to you.
