1. Who was thrown into the lions' den, for praying to God despite the king's decree?

Show answer

D) Daniel (Daniel 6:16). Daniel was thrown into the lions' den because he kept praying to his God three times a day, defying the king's decree.

2. Who were Daniel's three friends, thrown into the fiery furnace for refusing to bow to the golden statue?

Show answer

B) Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego (Daniel 3:19-23). Daniel's three friends were thrown into the blazing furnace for refusing to worship Nebuchadnezzar's golden image.

3. Which Babylonian king destroyed Jerusalem and the Temple, carrying the people into captivity?

Show answer

A) Nebuchadnezzar (2 Kings 25:8-10). Nebuchadnezzar burned the LORD's Temple, tore down the walls of Jerusalem, and carried the remaining people into exile in Babylon.

4. Who was the Jewish queen, wife of the Persian king Ahasuerus, who saved her people from destruction?

Show answer

D) Esther (Esther 2:17; 7:3-4). Esther, taken by the king as his wife, boldly interceded for her people, saving them from destruction.

5. What did King Belshazzar see written on the wall, during a feast, foretelling his downfall?

Show answer

D) A hand writing on the wall (Daniel 5:5). At that moment the fingers of a human hand appeared and wrote on the plaster of the wall, near the lampstand in the royal palace.

6. Who was seen as a "fourth" man in the fiery furnace, alongside the three friends, unharmed?

Show answer

A) An angel, "like a son of the gods" (Daniel 3:25). "I see four men walking around in the fire, unbound and unharmed, and the fourth looks like a son of the gods."

7. What happened to the clothes and smell of the three friends, after they came out unharmed from the fiery furnace?

Show answer

B) Not even the smell of fire was on them (Daniel 3:27). The hair of their heads was not singed, their robes were unchanged, and there was no smell of fire on them.

8. What happened to the mighty soldiers who threw the three friends into the fiery furnace?

Show answer

B) They themselves died from the heat of the fire (Daniel 3:22). The flames of the fire killed the soldiers who took up Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego.

9. What was Esther's name at birth, before she received her Persian name?

Show answer

C) Hadassah (Esther 2:7). He was bringing up Hadassah, that is Esther, his cousin's daughter.

10. Which queen was removed, for refusing to come at the command of King Ahasuerus, making way for Esther?

Show answer

D) Vashti (Esther 1:12). Queen Vashti refused to come at the king's command... the king became furious.

11. What did Persian law say about entering the king's presence unannounced, under penalty of death?

Show answer

B) Anyone who entered unsummoned risked death, unless the king extended his gold scepter (Esther 4:11). "There is one law for anyone, man or woman, who approaches the king in the inner court without being summoned. The king puts to death anyone who does so, unless the king holds out the gold scepter."

12. What did Esther say, deciding to go to the king uninvited, risking her life for her people?

Show answer

B) "If I perish, I perish" (Esther 4:16). "I will go to the king, even though it is against the law. And if I perish, I perish."

13. Why did King Ahasuerus summon Queen Vashti to the banquet, a request she refused?

Show answer

B) To display her beauty before the guests, wearing her royal crown (Esther 1:11). He commanded... to bring before him Queen Vashti, wearing her royal crown, in order to display her beauty to the people and nobles.

14. What food did Daniel choose to eat at the Babylonian court, instead of the king's royal food?

Show answer

A) Vegetables and water (Daniel 1:12). Daniel resolved not to defile himself, asking to be given only vegetables to eat and water to drink.

15. What huge statue did Nebuchadnezzar dream of, which Daniel interpreted as representing future kingdoms?

Show answer

B) One with a head of gold, chest of silver, belly of bronze, legs of iron and clay (Daniel 2:31-33). The head of the statue was made of pure gold, its chest and arms of silver, its belly and thighs of bronze, its legs of iron, its feet partly of iron and partly of clay.

16. What punishment did Nebuchadnezzar suffer for his pride, living like an animal for seven years?

Show answer

A) He ate grass like cattle, with his mind changed (Daniel 4:32-33). Nebuchadnezzar was driven away from people and ate grass like cattle, until his sanity was restored by God.

17. Who interpreted the handwriting on the wall for King Belshazzar, on the night of Babylon's fall?

Show answer

B) Daniel (Daniel 5:25-28). Daniel was summoned to read and interpret the writing, foretelling Babylon's fall that very night.

18. How many years did Jeremiah predict the people's captivity in Babylon would last?

Show answer

A) 70 years (Jeremiah 25:11-12; 29:10). "This whole country will become a desolate wasteland... seventy years," Jeremiah prophesied.

19. Who was forced, to his humiliation, to publicly honor Mordecai, leading him through the city on horseback?

Show answer

C) Haman (Esther 6:10-11). So Haman took the robe and the horse, arrayed Mordecai, and led him on horseback through the city, proclaiming: "This is what is done for the man the king delights to honor!"

20. How much hotter did Nebuchadnezzar order the fiery furnace heated, out of rage?

Show answer

C) Seven times hotter than usual (Daniel 3:19). He ordered the furnace heated seven times hotter than usual.

21. How tall was the golden statue set up by Nebuchadnezzar, which the people were required to worship?

Show answer

B) Sixty cubits (Daniel 3:1). King Nebuchadnezzar made an image of gold, sixty cubits high and six cubits wide.

22. What signal announced to the people that they must bow down to Nebuchadnezzar's golden statue?

Show answer

B) The sound of musical instruments (Daniel 3:5). "As soon as you hear the sound of the horn, flute, zither... you must fall down and worship the image of gold."

23. What did King Darius the Mede decree, for thirty days, regarding prayer?

Show answer

B) No one was to pray to any god or man except the king (Daniel 6:7). "Anyone who prays to any god or human being during the next thirty days, except to you, Your Majesty, shall be thrown into the lions' den."

24. What law could not be changed, not even by the king himself, sending Daniel to the lions' den?

Show answer

A) The law of the Medes and Persians (Daniel 6:15). "Remember, Your Majesty, that according to the law of the Medes and Persians no decree... can be changed."

25. What did the king use to seal the entrance of the lions' den where Daniel was thrown?

Show answer

B) A stone, sealed with the king's own signet and the signets of his nobles (Daniel 6:17). A stone was brought and placed over the mouth of the den, and the king sealed it with his own signet ring and with the rings of his nobles.

26. What did Daniel interpret from the word "Mene," written on the wall of Belshazzar's palace?

Show answer

A) That God has numbered the days of his reign and brought it to an end (Daniel 5:26). "This is the interpretation of these words: Mene means God has numbered the days of your reign and brought it to an end."
