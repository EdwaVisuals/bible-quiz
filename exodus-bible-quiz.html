1. Who led the people of Israel out of slavery in Egypt?

Show answer

D) Moses (Exodus 3:10). The LORD sent Moses to Pharaoh, to bring the people of Israel out of Egypt.

2. What was baby Moses placed in, to float on the waters of the Nile?

Show answer

B) A basket of papyrus reeds (Exodus 2:3). His mother took a papyrus basket, coated it with tar and pitch, and placed it among the reeds along the bank.

3. What was the name of Moses' sister, who watched over him by the riverbank?

Show answer

A) Miriam (Exodus 2:4,7; Numbers 26:59). Miriam, his sister, watched from a distance and arranged for their mother to nurse him.

4. What was the name of Moses' brother, who spoke for him before Pharaoh?

Show answer

B) Aaron (Exodus 4:14-16). The LORD appointed Aaron to speak in place of Moses, who considered himself slow of speech.

5. What did Moses see in the wilderness, burning without being consumed?

Show answer

C) The burning bush (Exodus 3:2). The angel of the LORD appeared to Moses in flames of fire from within a bush.

6. Into what did Moses' staff transform, as a sign given by God?

Show answer

D) A serpent (Exodus 4:2-4). Moses threw the staff on the ground, and it turned into a serpent.

7. What happened to Moses' hand, as the second sign given by God?

Show answer

D) It became covered with leprosy, then clean again (Exodus 4:6-7). Moses put his hand into his cloak, and when he took it out, it was leprous, as white as snow.

8. What happened to the waters of the Nile, in the first plague sent upon Egypt?

Show answer

A) They turned into blood (Exodus 7:20). All the water in the river was changed into blood.

9. What animals overran Egypt, in the second plague?

Show answer

B) Frogs (Exodus 8:6). Frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

10. What kind of bread were the Israelites to eat at the first Passover feast?

Show answer

A) Unleavened bread (Exodus 12:15). For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast; on the first day remove the yeast from your houses.

11. What birds did the LORD send, in the evening, as food for the Israelites in the wilderness?

Show answer

A) Quail (Exodus 16:13). That evening quail came and covered the camp.

12. What went ahead of the people by day, showing them the way through the wilderness?

Show answer

B) A pillar of cloud (Exodus 13:21). The LORD went ahead of them by day in a pillar of cloud, to guide them on their way.

13. What was the name of Moses' mother, who hid him for three months as a baby?

Show answer

C) Jochebed (Exodus 6:20). Amram married Jochebed... She bore him Aaron, Moses and Miriam.

14. How many plagues did God send upon Egypt, to force Pharaoh to free the people?

Show answer

C) 10 (Exodus 7–12). The LORD struck Egypt with ten plagues, until Pharaoh let the people go.

15. What was the last plague sent upon Egypt?

Show answer

C) The death of the firstborn (Exodus 11:5; 12:29). At midnight, the LORD struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt.

16. What sea parted, so the Israelites could cross on dry ground, pursued by Pharaoh's army?

Show answer

A) The Red Sea (Exodus 14:21-22). Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the waters parted, leaving dry ground.

17. What food did God send from heaven to the Israelites, every morning, in the wilderness?

Show answer

C) Manna (Exodus 16:14-15,31). In the morning, around the camp, there lay a thin flaky substance, like frost — manna.

18. On what mountain did Moses receive the Ten Commandments?

Show answer

C) Mount Sinai (Exodus 19:20; 20:1). The LORD descended on Mount Sinai and called Moses up to the top of the mountain.

19. What did Pharaoh's magicians do, imitating the first plague of water turned to blood?

Show answer

C) They did the same thing, through their secret arts (Exodus 7:22). The Egyptian magicians did the same things by their secret arts.

20. What plague could Pharaoh's magicians no longer reproduce, admitting it was "the finger of God"?

Show answer

B) The third plague, gnats (Exodus 8:18-19). The magicians said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." But they could not produce gnats.

21. What plague struck the flocks and herds of the Egyptians, sparing the Israelites' livestock?

Show answer

A) The fifth plague, a plague on livestock (Exodus 9:3-6). All the livestock of the Egyptians died, but not one animal belonging to the Israelites died.

22. What plague covered the bodies of the Egyptians and their magicians with painful sores?

Show answer

D) The sixth plague, boils (Exodus 9:9-10). Festering boils broke out on people and animals throughout the land of Egypt.

23. What plague destroyed everything still green in Egypt, mixed with fire?

Show answer

C) The seventh plague, hail (Exodus 9:23-25). Hail fell and lightning flashed back and forth, a storm as heavy as never before in all the land of Egypt.

24. What did Moses set aside, at Marah, to sweeten the bitter water for the people?

Show answer

A) A piece of wood shown by the LORD (Exodus 15:23-25). The LORD showed him a piece of wood; he threw it into the water, and the water became sweet.

25. What plague covered Egypt, darkening the land for three days, yet leaving light in Goshen?

Show answer

B) The ninth plague, darkness (Exodus 10:22-23). Total darkness covered all Egypt for three days; yet all the Israelites had light.

26. What did Pharaoh demand from the Hebrews, without providing the material needed, as punishment for asking to be freed?

Show answer

D) To make bricks without being given straw (Exodus 5:7). "You are no longer to supply the people with straw for making bricks; let them go and gather their own straw."
