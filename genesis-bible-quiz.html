1. In how many days did God create the world, according to Genesis, before resting?

Show answer

C) 6 days (Genesis 1:31; 2:2). God worked six days at creation, and on the seventh He rested.

2. Who was the first man created by God?

Show answer

B) Adam (Genesis 2:7). God formed Adam from the dust of the ground.

3. Who was the first woman, created from Adam's rib?

Show answer

A) Eve (Genesis 3:20). Eve was called "the mother of all living."

4. What was the garden called where Adam and Eve lived?

Show answer

B) Eden (Genesis 2:8). God planted a garden in Eden, in the east.

5. Who built the ark, at God's command?

Show answer

D) Noah (Genesis 6:14). Noah was commanded to build an ark of gopher wood.

6. On what day of creation was light made, according to Genesis?

Show answer

A) The first day (Genesis 1:3-5). God said, "Let there be light," and there was light, on the first day of creation.

7. Who named all the animals, at God's command?

Show answer

D) Adam (Genesis 2:19-20). The LORD God brought all the animals to Adam, so he could name them.

8. What was the tree in the middle of the Garden of Eden called, from which God commanded not to eat?

Show answer

B) The tree of the knowledge of good and evil (Genesis 2:16-17). The LORD commanded: "You must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil."

9. Who tempted Adam and Eve to eat from the forbidden tree?

Show answer

A) The serpent (Genesis 3:1). The serpent was more crafty than any beast of the field, and it tempted Eve.

10. What did God place at the entrance to Eden, after the fall, to guard it?

Show answer

B) Cherubim with a flaming sword (Genesis 3:24). God placed cherubim on the east side of the Garden of Eden, and a flaming sword flashing back and forth.

11. How many of Noah's sons boarded the ark, together with him?

Show answer

C) 3 (Genesis 6:10). Noah had three sons — Shem, Ham and Japheth — who entered the ark with him.

12. What did Noah build after the floodwaters receded, as a sign of thanks?

Show answer

A) An altar (Genesis 8:20). Noah built an altar to the LORD and offered burnt offerings on it.

13. What did God command mankind, as a blessing repeated even after the flood?

Show answer

A) Be fruitful and fill the earth (Genesis 9:1). God blessed Noah and his sons: "Be fruitful and increase in number and fill the earth."

14. For how many days and nights did it rain during the flood?

Show answer

C) 40 days (Genesis 7:12). The rain lasted forty days and forty nights.

15. Who was the first son of Adam and Eve?

Show answer

C) Cain (Genesis 4:1). Cain was the firstborn, followed by his brother Abel.

16. Whom did Cain kill, out of envy?

Show answer

D) Abel (Genesis 4:8). Cain grew angry because Abel's offering was accepted and his was not.

17. What sign did God set in the clouds after the flood, as a sign of His covenant with the earth?

Show answer

C) A rainbow (Genesis 9:13). The rainbow was given as a sign that the waters would never again destroy all the earth.

18. What bird sent out by Noah returned with an olive leaf in its beak?

Show answer

C) The dove (Genesis 8:11). The dove showed that the waters had receded from the earth.

19. What did God say He would do to the woman, as punishment for sin?

Show answer

C) She would give birth in pain (Genesis 3:16). God said to the woman, "I will make your pains in childbearing very severe; with painful labor you will give birth to children."

20. What punishment did the ground receive, because of Adam's sin?

Show answer

D) It would produce thorns and thistles (Genesis 3:17-18). "Cursed is the ground because of you... thorns and thistles it will produce for you."

21. What mark did God put on Cain, after he killed Abel, so no one who found him would kill him?

Show answer

B) A mark of protection (Genesis 4:15). The LORD put a mark on Cain, so that no one who found him would kill him.

22. What did Cain build, after being banished, naming it after his son?

Show answer

A) A city, named Enoch (Genesis 4:17). Cain built a city and named it after his son Enoch.

23. Who was Cain's great-great-grandson, known for a boastful song of revenge?

Show answer

C) Lamech (Genesis 4:23-24). Lamech, Cain's descendant, boasted to his wives that he had avenged himself seventy-seven times over.

24. Who was the ancestor who "walked with God" and was taken by God, without dying?

Show answer

C) Enoch (Genesis 5:24). Enoch walked faithfully with God; then he was no more, because God took him away.

25. Who were the Nephilim (giants), mentioned just before the flood?

Show answer

B) The offspring of the "sons of God" and the "daughters of men" (Genesis 6:2-4). The sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful... the Nephilim were on the earth in those days.

26. From what wood did God command Noah to build the ark?

Show answer

C) Gopher wood (Genesis 6:14). "Make yourself an ark of gopher wood," the LORD commanded Noah.
