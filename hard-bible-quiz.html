1. How many years did Methuselah live, the longest-lived man mentioned in Genesis?

Show answer

A) 969 years (Genesis 5:27). Methuselah lived nine hundred and sixty-nine years.

2. What dietary prohibition did God give mankind, after the flood, concerning blood?

Show answer

B) Not to eat meat with its blood still in it (Genesis 9:4). "You must not eat meat that has its lifeblood still in it."

3. Who was Rebekah's father?

Show answer

B) Bethuel (Genesis 24:15). Rebekah was the daughter of Bethuel.

4. What happened to Ishmael and Hagar in the wilderness, after being sent away by Abraham, but God helped them?

Show answer

B) They grew thirsty, and God showed them a well (Genesis 21:19). God opened her eyes and she saw a well of water.

5. What object did Joseph hide in Benjamin's sack, to test his brothers?

Show answer

B) A silver cup (Genesis 44:2). Joseph ordered his silver cup to be placed in the youngest brother's sack.

6. What did Joseph say to his brothers about the harm they had done him, as he revealed himself?

Show answer

A) "You intended to harm me, but God turned it into good" (Genesis 50:20). "You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good."

7. Who was ordained as the first high priest of Israel, with special garments for his service?

Show answer

B) Aaron (Exodus 28:1). "Have Aaron your brother brought to you... so he may serve me as priest."

8. What feast was instituted on the night of the last plague, when the Israelites put the blood of a lamb on their doorframes?

Show answer

A) The Passover (Exodus 12:1-13). The blood of the lamb, put on the two doorposts and the lintel, was a sign for the destroying angel to pass over.

9. How many times did Balak take Balaam to another place to curse Israel, receiving a blessing each time instead?

Show answer

A) Three times (Numbers 23-24). Balak took Balaam to three different places, but each time Balaam spoke a blessing over Israel.

10. What did Moses prophesy about a future prophet like himself, whom God would raise up?

Show answer

A) "The LORD will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you" (Deuteronomy 18:15). The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your own brothers.

11. How was the rest of the land divided, among the seven tribes left without an inheritance?

Show answer

B) By casting lots, at Shiloh (Joshua 18:10). Joshua cast lots for them at Shiloh, in the presence of the LORD, and there he distributed the land to the Israelites according to their tribal divisions.

12. What city did Joshua ask for himself, as an inheritance, at the end of the land's division?

Show answer

D) Timnath Serah, in the hill country of Ephraim (Joshua 19:49-50). They gave him the city he asked for — Timnath Serah in the hill country of Ephraim. He built up the city and settled there.

13. Who is the only woman explicitly named a judge of Israel, in the book of Judges?

Show answer

D) Deborah (Judges 4:4). Deborah is the only woman explicitly named a judge of Israel in the book of Judges.

14. Why was Jephthah banished by his brothers, before becoming leader of Gilead?

Show answer

C) He was the son of a prostitute, not his father's legitimate wife (Judges 11:1-2). Jephthah was the son of a prostitute... his half-brothers drove him away, saying, "You will not get any inheritance in our family."

15. How did the Philistines send the LORD's ark back, after their cities were struck with plagues?

Show answer

A) On a new cart, pulled by two nursing cows (1 Samuel 6:7-12). The Philistines put the ark on a new cart pulled by nursing cows, to see if the plague had truly come from the LORD.

16. Who wisely intervened, keeping David from taking revenge on her husband, the foolish Nabal?

Show answer

A) Abigail (1 Samuel 25:18-33). Abigail, Nabal's wife, hurried to bring David provisions, calming his anger and keeping him from bloodshed.

17. Who was Amasa, appointed by Absalom as commander of his rebel army, later killed treacherously by Joab?

Show answer

A) A relative of David, who sided with Absalom (2 Samuel 17:25; 20:9-10). Absalom had put Amasa in command of the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man named Jether... Joab struck him down with his sword.

18. What did the LORD promise David, through the prophet Nathan, about his house and throne, though He would not let him build the Temple?

Show answer

B) That his throne would endure forever (2 Samuel 7:12-16). The LORD promised David, through Nathan, an eternal covenant: his house, throne and kingdom would endure forever.

19. What did Solomon build at Ezion Geber, on the shore of the Red Sea, with the help of Hiram's men?

Show answer

C) A fleet of ships (1 Kings 9:26-27). King Solomon also built ships at Ezion Geber... Hiram sent his men — sailors who knew the sea — to serve in the fleet.

20. What ultimately led Solomon's heart into idolatry, in his old age?

Show answer

D) His many foreign wives, who turned his heart to other gods (1 Kings 11:3-4). Solomon had seven hundred wives and three hundred concubines, and they turned his heart after other gods, in his old age.

21. What lesson did Jeremiah learn at the potter's house, about God's power over nations?

Show answer

C) The parable of the potter reshaping the spoiled clay, according to his will (Jeremiah 18:1-6). "Can I not do with you, Israel, as this potter does?... Like clay in the hand of the potter, so are you in my hand."

22. What did the LORD command Ezekiel to eat, as a sign of his message?

Show answer

B) A scroll (a book) (Ezekiel 3:1-3). The LORD held out a scroll to Ezekiel and said, "Son of man, eat what is before you, eat this scroll."

23. What position did Nebuchadnezzar give Daniel, after he interpreted the dream of the four-metal statue?

Show answer

C) Ruler over the entire province of Babylon (Daniel 2:48). The king placed Daniel in a high position and lavished many gifts on him; he made him ruler over the entire province of Babylon.

24. What did King Darius do, worried, on the night Daniel was in the lions' den?

Show answer

D) He could not sleep and he fasted (Daniel 6:18). Then the king returned to his palace and spent the night without eating and without any entertainment being brought to him. And he could not sleep.

25. What vision did the prophet Zechariah have, of a gold lampstand with two olive trees beside it?

Show answer

C) The gold lampstand with two olive trees, symbol of the Spirit of the LORD (Zechariah 4:2-6). I see a solid gold lampstand... with two olive trees by it. ... "Not by might nor by power, but by my Spirit," says the LORD.

26. What ceremony was held at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem, with two choirs of thanksgiving?

Show answer

D) Two processions of singers, walking on the wall in opposite directions, giving thanks (Nehemiah 12:31,38). I appointed two large choirs to give thanks... one was to proceed on top of the wall to the right, the other to the left.

27. Why did Jesus's family settle in Nazareth, upon returning from Egypt, instead of Judea?

Show answer

C) Joseph was afraid of Archelaus, Herod's son, who ruled there (Matthew 2:22). Joseph was afraid to go there, and having been warned in a dream, he withdrew to the district of Galilee.

28. What sorrowful prophecy did Simeon tell Mary, about a future suffering connected to Jesus?

Show answer

D) A sword will pierce her soul (Luke 2:34-35). Simeon said to Mary, his mother, "And a sword will pierce your own soul too, so that the thoughts of many hearts will be revealed."

29. What did Martha say to Jesus, professing her faith, right before Lazarus's resurrection?

Show answer

D) "I believe that you are the Messiah, the Son of God" (John 11:27). "Yes, Lord," she replied, "I believe that you are the Messiah, the Son of God, who is to come into the world."

30. What did Jesus say about the Sabbath, defending his disciples who were picking heads of grain on a Sabbath day?

Show answer

D) "The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath" (Mark 2:27). The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath.

31. What dream did Pilate's wife have, prompting her to warn her husband about Jesus?

Show answer

C) She suffered greatly in a dream because of "that innocent man" (Matthew 27:19). His wife sent him this message: "Don't have anything to do with that innocent man, for I have suffered a great deal today in a dream because of him."

32. To what village were two disciples walking when the risen Jesus joined them, without their recognizing him at first?

Show answer

C) Emmaus (Luke 24:13-16). Now that same day two of them were going to a village called Emmaus.

33. Who guarded the clothes of those who stoned Stephen?

Show answer

D) Saul (Acts 7:58). The witnesses laid their coats at the feet of a young man named Saul.

34. What did Stephen say, in his sermon before the Sanhedrin, that enraged his listeners?

Show answer

A) He rebuked them for resisting the Holy Spirit, just like their ancestors (Acts 7:51-54). You stiff-necked people... you always resist the Holy Spirit! Just as your ancestors did, so do you.

35. On what ship did Paul continue toward Rome, after wintering on Malta, following the shipwreck?

Show answer

C) A ship from Alexandria, with the figurehead of the twin gods (Castor and Pollux) (Acts 28:11). After three months we put out to sea in a ship that had wintered in the island. It was an Alexandrian ship with the figurehead of the twin gods Castor and Pollux.

36. What happened to Paul and Barnabas at Lystra, first taken for gods, then Paul stoned?

Show answer

C) They were taken for Zeus and Hermes, then Paul was stoned and left for dead (Acts 14:11-19). The crowd... shouted, "The gods have come down to us in human form!"... then the Jews stirred up the crowds and stoned Paul.

37. What warning does Paul give the church in Galatia, about another gospel?

Show answer

A) That whoever preaches another gospel is to be cursed (Galatians 1:8-9). Even if we or an angel from heaven should preach a gospel other than the one we preached to you, let them be under God's curse!

38. What does 2 Peter write about God's patience, concerning the fulfillment of his promises?

Show answer

A) The Lord is not slow but is patient, not wanting anyone to perish (2 Peter 3:9). The Lord is not slow in keeping his promise... instead he is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish, but everyone to come to repentance.

39. What is the name of the church described as "neither cold nor hot," sharply rebuked by Christ?

Show answer

D) Laodicea (Revelation 3:15-16). So, because you are lukewarm — neither hot nor cold — I am about to spit you out of my mouth, the church of Laodicea was told.

40. What weapon came out of the mouth of the rider on the white horse, to strike down the nations?

Show answer

B) A sharp sword (Revelation 19:15). Coming out of his mouth is a sharp sword with which to strike down the nations.
