1. Who was Joseph's father?

B) Jacob (Genesis 37:2-3). Joseph was the son of Jacob, born to Rachel.

2. What special garment did Jacob give Joseph, which stirred his brothers' envy?

Show answer

C) A richly ornamented coat of many colors (Genesis 37:3). Israel made Joseph a richly ornamented coat, a sign that he loved him more than his other sons.

3. What did Joseph's brothers do to him, out of envy?

Show answer

B) They sold him as a slave (Genesis 37:28). They pulled Joseph out of the pit and sold him to Ishmaelite traders.

4. To what country was Joseph taken, as a slave?

Show answer

D) Egypt (Genesis 37:36). The traders took Joseph to Egypt.

5. What was the name of Joseph's mother?

Show answer

A) Rachel (Genesis 30:22-24). Rachel bore Jacob a son, Joseph.

6. What did Joseph dream about sheaves of grain, which angered his brothers?

Show answer

C) His sheaf stood upright, and his brothers' sheaves bowed down to it (Genesis 37:7). "My sheaf rose and stood upright, while your sheaves gathered around mine and bowed down to it."

7. What did Joseph dream a second time, about the sun, moon and stars?

Show answer

C) They bowed down before him (Genesis 37:9). "The sun and moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me."

8. Which brother proposed selling Joseph, instead of killing him?

Show answer

B) Judah (Genesis 37:26-27). Judah said to his brothers, "What will we gain if we kill our brother?... Let's sell him to the Ishmaelites."

9. For how many pieces of silver did the brothers sell Joseph to the traders?

Show answer

A) Twenty shekels of silver (Genesis 37:28). They sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty shekels of silver.

10. What did Jacob believe had happened to Joseph, seeing the blood-stained coat?

Show answer

B) That he had been torn apart by a wild animal (Genesis 37:33). Jacob said, "My son's robe is torn; a wild animal has devoured him."

11. What words did Joseph use to reveal himself to his brothers, in Egypt?

Show answer

A) "I am Joseph, your brother" (Genesis 45:3). Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph! Is my father still living?"

12. How many of Jacob's sons first went down to Egypt to buy grain?

Show answer

B) Ten, without Benjamin (Genesis 42:3-4). Joseph's ten brothers went down to Egypt to buy grain; Benjamin was not sent with them.

13. What did Pharaoh give Joseph, as a sign of authority, when he appointed him governor?

Show answer

D) His signet ring (Genesis 41:42). Pharaoh took his signet ring off his finger and put it on Joseph's finger.

14. For whom was Joseph bought, as a slave, in Egypt?

Show answer

B) Potiphar, one of Pharaoh's officials (Genesis 37:36). Potiphar, one of Pharaoh's officials, bought Joseph from the Ishmaelites.

15. Why was Joseph imprisoned in Egypt?

Show answer

B) Potiphar's wife falsely accused him (Genesis 39:20). Potiphar's wife falsely accused him, and Joseph was thrown into prison.

16. What did Joseph interpret in prison, for Pharaoh's cupbearer and baker?

Show answer

A) Their dreams (Genesis 40:8). "Do not interpretations belong to God? Tell me your dreams," Joseph said.

17. How many fat cows and how many lean cows did Pharaoh dream of?

Show answer

B) 7 and 7 (Genesis 41:2-4). Pharaoh dreamed of seven fat cows and seven lean cows coming up out of the Nile.

18. What did Joseph foretell through Pharaoh's dream of the fat and lean cows?

Show answer

D) Seven years of abundance followed by seven years of famine (Genesis 41:29-30). Joseph explained that Egypt would have seven years of abundance, followed by seven years of famine.

19. What did Joseph leave in the hand of Potiphar's wife, fleeing from her?

Show answer

C) His cloak (Genesis 39:12). She caught him by his cloak... he left his cloak in her hand and ran.

20. How long did the cupbearer forget Joseph, after he was released from prison?

Show answer

C) Two years (Genesis 41:1,9). The chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph... two full years had passed.

21. What did Pharaoh see in his second dream, alongside the fat and lean cows?

Show answer

A) Seven full and seven withered heads of grain (Genesis 41:5-7). Seven heads of grain, healthy and good... and after them, seven other heads, thin and scorched by the east wind.

22. What does the name Manasseh mean, given by Joseph to his first son?

Show answer

A) "God has made me forget my trouble" (Genesis 41:51). He named his firstborn Manasseh, saying, "God has made me forget all my trouble."

23. What does the name Ephraim mean, given by Joseph to his second son?

Show answer

A) "God has made me fruitful in the land of my suffering" (Genesis 41:52). He named his second son Ephraim, saying, "God has made me fruitful in the land of my suffering."

24. How did Joseph gather grain during the seven years of abundance?

Show answer

A) Like the sand of the sea, so much he stopped measuring it (Genesis 41:49). Joseph stored up grain in great abundance, like the sand of the sea, until he stopped measuring it.

25. Why did Joseph accuse his brothers, on their first visit to Egypt, to test them?

Show answer

C) Of being spies (Genesis 42:9). Joseph said to them, "You are spies! You have come to see where our land is unprotected."

26. What portion did Benjamin receive at the feast Joseph gave his brothers, larger than the others'?

Show answer

D) Five times as much (Genesis 43:34). Benjamin's portion was five times as much as anyone else's.
