1. What was the period called in which Israel was led by temporary deliverers, before the kings?

Show answer

B) The period of the Judges (Judges 2:16). After Joshua's death, the LORD raised up judges from time to time, to deliver the people from their oppressors.

2. Who was a woman judge and prophetess, who led Israel to victory alongside Barak?

Show answer

B) Deborah (Judges 4:4-6). Deborah, a prophetess, was judging Israel at that time and called Barak to battle.

3. Who was a judge known for extraordinary strength, who lost his power once his hair was cut?

Show answer

C) Samson (Judges 16:17). Samson's strength lay in his Nazirite vow, marked by his uncut hair.

4. Who killed Sisera, the enemy general, by driving a tent peg through his temple?

Show answer

A) Jael (Judges 4:21). Jael welcomed Sisera into her tent, and while he slept, she drove a tent peg through his temple.

5. Who was the Moabite woman who stayed with her mother-in-law Naomi, becoming David's great-grandmother?

Show answer

A) Ruth (Ruth 1:16). Ruth, the Moabite, told her mother-in-law: "Your people will be my people and your God my God."

6. Who was Shamgar, a judge who killed 600 Philistines with an oxgoad?

Show answer

A) The son of Anath, a judge of Israel (Judges 3:31). After him came Shamgar son of Anath, who struck down six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad. He too saved Israel.

7. Under what kind of tree did Deborah sit, when she judged the people of Israel?

Show answer

D) A palm tree (Judges 4:5). She held court under the Palm of Deborah, between Ramah and Bethel, in the hill country of Ephraim, and the Israelites came to her to have their disputes decided.

8. What condition did Barak set, to go into battle against Sisera?

Show answer

A) That Deborah go with him (Judges 4:8). Barak said, "If you go with me, I will go; but if you don't go with me, I won't go."

9. What was Gideon doing when the angel of the LORD found him, hiding from the Midianites?

Show answer

A) Threshing wheat in a winepress (Judges 6:11). The angel of the LORD found Gideon threshing wheat in a winepress, to keep it from the Midianites.

10. How did the angel of the LORD address Gideon, at his calling?

Show answer

A) "Mighty warrior" (Judges 6:12). The angel of the LORD appeared to him and said, "The LORD is with you, mighty warrior."

11. What did Gideon destroy at night, by the LORD's command, in his father's yard?

Show answer

B) The altar of Baal (Judges 6:25-27). That same night the LORD said to him, "Tear down your father's altar to Baal."

12. Who were Samson's parents, visited by an angel who foretold the birth of a son?

Show answer

C) Manoah and his wife (Judges 13:2-3). The angel of the LORD appeared to the woman and said, "You are barren and childless, but you are going to become pregnant and give birth to a son."

13. What restrictions did Samson have from birth, as a Nazirite dedicated to God?

Show answer

B) Not to drink wine, not to touch anything unclean, not to cut his hair (Judges 13:4-5). The boy is to be dedicated to God from the womb... no razor may be used on his head.

14. With what unusual weapon did Samson kill a thousand Philistines?

Show answer

A) The jawbone of a donkey (Judges 15:15-16). Samson found a fresh jawbone of a donkey and killed a thousand men with it.

15. Who was the Philistine woman who deceived Samson, learning the secret of his strength?

Show answer

D) Delilah (Judges 16:4-18). Delilah persuaded Samson, through persistent nagging, to reveal the secret of his strength.

16. What sign did Gideon ask of the LORD, to be sure of his calling — wet fleece and dry ground, then the reverse?

Show answer

C) The sign of the fleece (Judges 6:36-40). Gideon asked for the fleece to be wet with dew while the ground stayed dry — and then the reverse — to be sure of God's calling.

17. To how many men did the LORD reduce Gideon's army, before the battle with the Midianites, so the victory would be His?

Show answer

B) 300 (Judges 7:2-7). The LORD reduced Gideon's army to three hundred men, so Israel could not boast that it had saved itself.

18. What rash vow did Jephthah make before battle, which led to great tragedy?

Show answer

A) That he would sacrifice the first thing to come out of his house (Judges 11:30-35). Jephthah promised to sacrifice to the LORD whatever came out of his house to meet him upon his return — and his daughter was the one who came out.

19. What new name was Gideon given, after he tore down the altar of Baal?

Show answer

A) Jerub-Baal, "let Baal contend" (Judges 6:32). That day they called Gideon "Jerub-Baal," saying, "Let Baal contend with him," because he broke down his altar.

20. How were Gideon's 300 men chosen, from those who remained?

Show answer

D) By the way they drank water from the river — lapping like a dog (Judges 7:5-7). "Separate those who lap the water with their tongues as a dog laps from those who kneel down to drink."

21. What dream did Gideon overhear in the Midianite camp, which encouraged him before battle?

Show answer

B) A loaf of barley bread tumbling into a tent (Judges 7:13). A man was telling his friend his dream... "A round loaf of barley bread came tumbling into the Midianite camp and struck the tent."

22. What unusual weapons did Gideon's 300 men use, in the night attack?

Show answer

A) Trumpets, empty jars and torches (Judges 7:19-20). The three companies blew the trumpets and broke the jars; they held the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right.

23. What did the ephod Gideon made from the gold of the plunder become, to the harm of his family?

Show answer

B) A snare of idolatry for the people and his household (Judges 8:27). Gideon made the gold into an ephod... All Israel prostituted themselves by worshiping it, and it became a snare to Gideon and his family.

24. Who killed 70 of his brothers on a single stone, to remain the sole ruler?

Show answer

B) Abimelech (Judges 9:5). He went to his father's home at Ophrah and murdered his seventy brothers, the sons of Jerub-Baal, on a single stone.

25. What parable did Jotham, the only brother who escaped Abimelech, tell about trees choosing a king?

Show answer

C) The parable of the trees seeking a king among themselves (Judges 9:7-15). The trees went out to anoint a king for themselves... they said to the olive tree, "Be our king."

26. What did the Philistines do to Samson's wife and her father, in revenge for the foxes with torches?

Show answer

C) They burned them alive (Judges 15:6). The Philistines went up and burned her and her father to death.
