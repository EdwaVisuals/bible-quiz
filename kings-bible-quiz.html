1. Who was Samuel's mother, who prayed to God for a long time for a son?

Show answer

C) Hannah (1 Samuel 1:10-11,20). Hannah, Elkanah's wife, prayed with a bitter soul for a son and dedicated him to the LORD.

2. Whom did Samuel serve, as a boy, in the LORD's Tent at Shiloh?

Show answer

D) The priest Eli (1 Samuel 1:24-28). Hannah brought young Samuel to the priest Eli, so he could serve the LORD there for his whole life.

3. Who was the first king of Israel, anointed by Samuel?

Show answer

D) Saul (1 Samuel 10:1). Samuel anointed Saul, of the tribe of Benjamin, as the first king of Israel.

4. What sacred object was captured by the Philistines in battle, to Israel's great shame?

Show answer

D) The ark of the covenant (1 Samuel 4:10-11). The ark of the covenant was taken by the Philistines, and Eli's two sons died in battle.

5. What was young Saul looking for, when he first met Samuel?

Show answer

B) His father's donkeys (1 Samuel 9:3-17). Saul was out looking for his father Kish's stray donkeys, when God guided him to Samuel.

6. Who was Peninnah, Elkanah's other wife, who provoked Hannah because she had no children?

Show answer

A) Elkanah's second wife, who had children (1 Samuel 1:2,6). Peninnah, who had children, would provoke Hannah, to make her feel the LORD had left her childless.

7. What garment did Hannah sew, every year, for her son Samuel, who served at Shiloh?

Show answer

C) A little robe (1 Samuel 2:19). Each year his mother made him a little robe and took it to him when she went up with her husband to offer the sacrifice.

8. What did Samuel answer, taught by Eli, when the LORD called him again, in the night?

Show answer

B) "Speak, LORD, for your servant is listening" (1 Samuel 3:9). Eli told Samuel, "Go and lie down, and if he calls you, say, 'Speak, LORD, for your servant is listening.'"

9. What name was given to Eli's grandson, born on the very day the ark was captured, meaning "the glory has departed"?

Show answer

A) Ichabod (1 Samuel 4:21). She named the boy Ichabod, saying, "The glory has departed from Israel" — because the ark of God had been captured.

10. With what painful disease were the inhabitants of the Philistine cities struck, where the LORD's ark stayed?

Show answer

A) Tumors (painful growths) (1 Samuel 5:6). The LORD's hand was heavy on the people of Ashdod... he afflicted them with tumors.

11. What golden gifts did the Philistines send back with the ark, as a guilt offering?

Show answer

D) Five gold tumors and five gold rats (1 Samuel 6:4). "Send back... five gold tumors and five gold rats, according to the number of the Philistine rulers."

12. How was Saul's stature described, compared to the rest of the people?

Show answer

B) A head taller than anyone else in the nation (1 Samuel 9:2). There was not a young man more handsome than he; he was a head taller than anyone else.

13. What did Samuel command the Israelites to do with foreign idols, before the victory at Mizpah?

Show answer

C) Get rid of the Baals and the Ashtoreths (1 Samuel 7:3). Samuel said, "If you are returning to the LORD with all your hearts, then rid yourselves of the foreign gods and the Ashtoreths."

14. At what city was Saul publicly reconfirmed as king, after the victory over the Ammonites?

Show answer

D) Gilgal (1 Samuel 11:14-15). Samuel said to the people, "Come, let us go to Gilgal and there renew the kingship."

15. Why did the people of Israel ask for a king, in Samuel's old age?

Show answer

D) To be like all the other nations (1 Samuel 8:5-7). The elders of Israel asked Samuel for a king "such as all the other nations have," which grieved the LORD.

16. Where had Saul hidden, out of shyness, when he was chosen king by lot?

Show answer

B) Among the baggage (1 Samuel 10:20-23). Saul had hidden among the baggage, and the people had to search for the one the LORD had chosen.

17. What did Saul do at Gilgal, in his impatience, which drew a sharp rebuke from Samuel?

Show answer

D) He offered the sacrifice himself, in place of the priest (1 Samuel 13:8-13). Saul did not wait for Samuel to arrive and offered the burnt offering himself, breaking the LORD's command.

18. What did the LORD command Saul to do with the Amalekites and their possessions, a command Saul did not fully carry out?

Show answer

C) Destroy them completely, people and animals (1 Samuel 15:3,9). Saul spared Agag, king of the Amalekites, and the best of the animals, though the LORD had commanded total destruction.

19. What tormented Saul's spirit, after the Spirit of the LORD departed from him?

Show answer

B) A distressing spirit, which troubled him (1 Samuel 16:14-15). A distressing spirit, allowed by the LORD, tormented Saul, and David's music soothed him.

20. What duty did Samuel perform, year after year, traveling between several cities of Israel?

Show answer

D) He judged the people, going to Bethel, Gilgal and Mizpah (1 Samuel 7:15-16). Year after year he went on a circuit from Bethel to Gilgal to Mizpah, judging Israel in all those places.

21. Who were Samuel's sons, appointed judges but corrupt, the reason for the demand for a king?

Show answer

D) Joel and Abijah (1 Samuel 8:1-5). His sons did not follow his ways... they turned aside after dishonest gain and accepted bribes; so the elders demanded a king.

22. What did the people exclaim, astonished, seeing Saul prophesying among the prophets?

Show answer

B) "Is Saul also among the prophets?" (1 Samuel 10:11). Those who had known him previously said, "What is this that has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

23. Against whom did Saul win his first great victory as king, saving the city of Jabesh Gilead?

Show answer

D) Nahash, king of the Ammonites (1 Samuel 11:1,11). Nahash the Ammonite besieged Jabesh Gilead... Saul summoned the people and defeated the Ammonites.

24. What did Saul do with a pair of oxen, to urgently summon the people to fight the Ammonites?

Show answer

A) He cut them into pieces and sent them throughout the territory of Israel (1 Samuel 11:7). Saul took a pair of oxen, cut them into pieces, and sent the pieces by messengers throughout Israel.

25. Who accompanied Jonathan, Saul's son, in the daring attack on the Philistine garrison?

Show answer

C) His armor-bearer (1 Samuel 14:1,6). Jonathan said to his young armor-bearer, "Come, let's go over to the outpost of those uncircumcised men."

26. What rash oath did Saul make on the day of battle with the Philistines, unknowingly endangering Jonathan's life?

Show answer

C) He swore that no one would eat anything until evening (1 Samuel 14:24). Saul said, "Cursed be anyone who eats food before evening."

27. Who finally killed Agag, the king of the Amalekites whom Saul had spared?

Show answer

A) Samuel himself, at Gilgal (1 Samuel 15:32-33). Samuel put Agag to death before the LORD at Gilgal.

28. What did Samuel tell Saul, rebuking him for disobedience, about the value of obedience?

Show answer

B) "To obey is better than sacrifice" (1 Samuel 15:22). To obey is better than sacrifice, and to heed is better than the fat of rams.
