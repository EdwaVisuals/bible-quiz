1. Who baptized Jesus in the Jordan River?

Show answer

C) John the Baptist (Matthew 3:13-17). Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan, to John, to be baptized by him.

2. What was Jesus's first miracle, turning water into wine?

Show answer

A) At the wedding in Cana (John 2:1-11). This, the first of his signs, Jesus performed in Cana of Galilee.

3. How many disciples (apostles) did Jesus choose?

Show answer

D) 12 (Mark 3:14). He appointed twelve, that they might be with him and that he might send them out to preach.

4. Who was the apostle called "the rock," whose name was Simon?

Show answer

C) Peter (Matthew 16:18). "You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church," Jesus told Simon.

5. What did Jesus do with five loaves and two fish, feeding a great crowd?

Show answer

A) He multiplied the food, feeding 5,000 men (Matthew 14:17-21). They all ate and were satisfied, and the number of those who ate was about five thousand men.

6. What did Jesus say, when the disciples wanted to send away the children brought to him?

Show answer

C) "Let the little children come to me" (Mark 10:14). Jesus said, "Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these."

7. Who was the tax collector Jesus called, directly from the tax booth, to be his disciple?

Show answer

A) Matthew (Matthew 9:9). As Jesus went on, he saw a tax collector named Matthew sitting at the tax collector's booth. "Follow me," he told him.

8. What model prayer did Jesus teach the disciples, known as the "Lord's Prayer"?

Show answer

A) A prayer beginning "Our Father in heaven" (Matthew 6:9). This, then, is how you should pray: "Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name."

9. How many lepers did Jesus heal, though only one returned to thank him?

Show answer

C) Ten (Luke 17:12-15). Jesus met ten men who had leprosy... One of them, when he saw he was healed, came back, praising God in a loud voice.

10. What did Jesus use to heal a man born blind?

Show answer

C) He spit on the ground, made mud, and put it on the man's eyes (John 9:6). He spit on the ground, made some mud with the saliva, and put it on the man's eyes.

11. Who was the synagogue leader's daughter, raised by Jesus with the words "Talitha koum"?

Show answer

C) Jairus's daughter (Mark 5:41-42). He took her by the hand and said to her, "Talitha koum!" (which means "Little girl, I say to you, get up!").

12. What did a woman, sick for many years, touch in the crowd, to be healed by Jesus?

Show answer

B) The edge of his cloak (Matthew 9:20-21). She touched the edge of his cloak. She said to herself, "If I only touch his cloak, I will be healed."

13. What did Jesus say about himself, using the image of a shepherd caring for his flock?

Show answer

D) "I am the good shepherd" (John 10:11). "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep."

14. How many days did Jesus fast in the wilderness, being tempted by the devil?

Show answer

C) 40 days (Matthew 4:1-2). Jesus was led by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil, and he fasted forty days and forty nights.

15. Who was Jesus's friend, whom he raised from the dead, after four days in the tomb?

Show answer

C) Lazarus (John 11:39-44). Jesus called in a loud voice, "Lazarus, come out!" And the dead man came out.

16. What woman did Jesus meet at a well in Samaria, speaking to her of "living water"?

Show answer

B) A Samaritan woman (John 4:7-14). Jesus told the woman, "Whoever drinks the water I give them will never thirst."

17. Who was the short tax collector who climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus?

Show answer

A) Zacchaeus (Luke 19:2-4). Zacchaeus was short in stature and ran ahead, climbing a sycamore-fig tree to see Jesus.

18. What did Jesus do during a storm at sea, commanding the wind and waves?

Show answer

D) He calmed them, rebuking them (Mark 4:39). He got up, rebuked the wind and said to the waves, "Quiet! Be still!" Then the wind died down and it was completely calm.

19. Who came to Jesus at night, so as not to be seen by the other Pharisees, asking about being born again?

Show answer

D) Nicodemus (John 3:1-2). There was a Pharisee, a man named Nicodemus who was a member of the Jewish ruling council. He came to Jesus at night.

20. What did Jesus tell Nicodemus, about the condition necessary to see the kingdom of God?

Show answer

A) You must be born again (John 3:3). Jesus replied, "Very truly I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born again."

21. What action of Jesus stirred anger in the Temple, upon seeing the merchants and money changers?

Show answer

B) He overturned the tables and drove out the animals (John 2:15). He made a whip out of cords, and drove all of them out of the temple courts... he overturned the tables of the money changers.

22. About which man did Jesus say he had not found such great faith even in Israel?

Show answer

D) The Roman centurion, for his sick servant (Matthew 8:10). Jesus said to those following him, "Truly I tell you, I have not found anyone in Israel with such great faith."

23. What happened to Peter, walking on the water toward Jesus, at his command, but then doubting?

Show answer

B) He began to sink, out of fear (Matthew 14:30). But when he saw the wind, he was afraid and, beginning to sink, cried out, "Lord, save me!"

24. Which woman was praised by Jesus for her good choice, unlike her sister who was busy with many tasks?

Show answer

C) Mary, Martha's sister (Luke 10:41-42). Mary has chosen what is better, and it will not be taken away from her. "Martha, Martha, you are worried and upset about many things."

25. What parable did Jesus tell about a man robbed and beaten, helped by a compassionate stranger, while a priest and a Levite passed by?

Show answer

D) The parable of the Good Samaritan (Luke 10:33). A Samaritan, as he traveled, came where the man was; and when he saw him, he took pity on him.

26. What parable did Jesus tell about a lost sheep, which the shepherd searches for, leaving the other ninety-nine?

Show answer

A) The parable of the lost sheep (Luke 15:4). Suppose one of you has a hundred sheep and loses one of them. Doesn't he leave the ninety-nine and go after the lost sheep?
