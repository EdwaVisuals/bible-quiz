1. How many spies did Moses send to explore the land of Canaan?

Show answer

C) 12 (Numbers 13:1-16). Moses sent one man from each tribe — twelve spies — to explore Canaan.

2. Which two spies brought back a report of faith, unlike the other ten?

Show answer

A) Joshua and Caleb (Numbers 14:6-9). Only Joshua and Caleb believed that the LORD would give them the land, despite the giants they had seen there.

3. What animal spoke to Balaam on the road, stopping him in his tracks?

Show answer

A) His donkey (Numbers 22:28). The LORD opened the donkey's mouth, and she spoke to Balaam.

4. Who rebelled against the leadership of Moses and Aaron, together with 250 leaders?

Show answer

C) Korah (Numbers 16:1-3). Korah, Dathan and Abiram rose up against Moses, with 250 leaders of the assembly.

5. Who was Moses' sister, struck with leprosy for speaking against him?

Show answer

A) Miriam (Numbers 12:1-10). Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses, and Miriam was struck with leprosy.

6. What did the spies bring back from Canaan, as proof of the land's fruitfulness?

Show answer

B) A huge cluster of grapes (Numbers 13:23). They cut off a branch with a single cluster of grapes and carried it on a pole between two of them.

7. Who did the spies fear, among the inhabitants of Canaan?

Show answer

A) The Anakite giants (Numbers 13:33). "We saw the giants there, the descendants of Anak; we seemed like grasshoppers in our own eyes."

8. What did the people want to do, hearing the frightening report of the ten spies?

Show answer

B) Return to Egypt (Numbers 14:1-4). The whole assembly said, "Wouldn't it be better for us to die in Egypt?... Let's choose a leader and go back to Egypt."

9. What signaled to the people the moment to break camp and set out, through the wilderness?

Show answer

D) The cloud lifting above the Tabernacle (Numbers 9:17). Whenever the cloud lifted from above the tent, the Israelites set out.

10. With what were the assemblies of the people and the breaking of camp announced, through the wilderness?

Show answer

C) Silver trumpets (Numbers 10:2). "Make two trumpets of hammered silver... they are to be used for calling the community together."

11. What special vow could the Israelites take, which meant not cutting their hair and not drinking wine?

Show answer

A) The Nazirite vow (Numbers 6:2-3). A man or woman who takes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite... must not touch wine.

12. What did the people crave to eat in the wilderness, grumbling that the manna was no longer enough?

Show answer

D) Meat (Numbers 11:4-6). "Who will give us meat to eat? We remember the fish we ate in Egypt."

13. Who was Sihon, the king defeated by the Israelites before entering Canaan?

Show answer

A) The king of the Amorites (Numbers 21:21-24). Israel put Sihon king of the Amorites to the sword and took possession of his land.

14. Who was Og, the other king defeated by the Israelites, east of the Jordan?

Show answer

D) The king of Bashan (Numbers 21:33-35). The LORD said to Moses, "Do not be afraid of him, for I have delivered him into your hands." So they struck down Og king of Bashan.

15. How many years did the Israelites wander in the wilderness, because of their unbelief?

Show answer

C) 40 years (Numbers 14:33-34). The people wandered forty years in the wilderness, one year for each of the forty days the spies spent in Canaan.

16. Who was hired by Balak, king of Moab, to curse Israel?

Show answer

B) Balaam (Numbers 22:5-6). Balak, king of Moab, sent for Balaam, to curse the people of Israel.

17. What did Moses do at Kadesh, instead of speaking to the rock, angering God?

Show answer

B) He struck the rock twice with his staff (Numbers 20:10-11). Moses struck the rock twice with his staff, instead of speaking to it as the LORD had commanded.

18. What bronze symbol did Moses lift up on a pole, so that anyone bitten by a snake would live by looking at it?

Show answer

C) A bronze snake (Numbers 21:8-9). Moses made a bronze snake and put it on a pole; anyone who looked at it after being bitten lived.

19. What did God send the Israelites as punishment, for grumbling again in the wilderness?

Show answer

B) Venomous snakes (Numbers 21:6). The LORD sent venomous snakes among the people, and they bit them.

20. How many elders did Moses appoint, filled with the Spirit, to help him carry the burden of the people?

Show answer

A) 70 (Numbers 11:16). The LORD said to Moses, "Gather for me seventy of Israel's elders."

21. What was the name of the place where those who craved meat and were struck by a plague were buried?

Show answer

B) Kibroth Hattaavah ("graves of craving") (Numbers 11:34). That place was called Kibroth Hattaavah, because there they buried the people who had craved meat.

22. What did Balaam prophesy about a future sign, "a star will come out of Jacob"?

Show answer

D) That a star would come out of Jacob and a scepter out of Israel (Numbers 24:17). "A star will come out of Jacob; a scepter will rise out of Israel."

23. By what sin did Israel let itself be drawn in by the Moabite women, at Baal Peor?

Show answer

C) Idol worship and immorality (Numbers 25:1-3). The people began to indulge in sexual immorality with Moabite women... Israel yoked themselves to Baal of Peor.

24. Against which people did God send a war of vengeance, after the sin at Baal Peor?

Show answer

B) The Midianites (Numbers 31:1-2). The LORD said to Moses, "Take vengeance on the Midianites for the Israelites."

25. Which two and a half tribes asked to settle east of the Jordan, before entering Canaan?

Show answer

B) Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh (Numbers 32:1-5). The Reubenites and Gadites had very large herds and flocks... they asked for the land east of the Jordan.

26. How many cities of refuge, in total, were appointed for Israel?

Show answer

A) Six cities (Numbers 35:6). The cities you give the Levites will be six cities of refuge.
