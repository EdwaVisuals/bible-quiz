1. What animal did Jesus ride into Jerusalem, in triumph, acclaimed by the crowd?

Show answer

B) A young donkey (Matthew 21:7-9). The disciples brought the donkey and the colt, placed their cloaks on them, and Jesus sat on them.

2. Who betrayed Jesus, for thirty pieces of silver?

Show answer

B) Judas Iscariot (Matthew 26:14-15). Then one of the Twelve — the one called Judas Iscariot — said, "What are you willing to give me if I deliver him over to you?"

3. How many times did Peter deny Jesus, before the rooster crowed?

Show answer

A) Three times (Matthew 26:34,75). "Truly I tell you," Jesus answered, "this very night, before the rooster crows, you will disown me three times."

4. On what hill was Jesus crucified?

Show answer

A) Golgotha (Matthew 27:33). They came to a place called Golgotha, which means "the place of the skull."

5. On what day did Jesus rise from the dead, according to Scripture?

Show answer

D) The third day (Luke 24:7,46). He will be delivered over to the Gentiles... on the third day he will rise again.

6. What did the crowd shout, waving palm branches, at Jesus's triumphal entry into Jerusalem?

Show answer

C) "Hosanna!" (Matthew 21:9). The crowds that went ahead of him and those that followed shouted, "Hosanna to the Son of David!"

7. What did the crowd lay on the road, before Jesus, at his triumphal entry into Jerusalem?

Show answer

C) Cloaks and tree branches (Matthew 21:8). A very large crowd spread their cloaks on the road, while others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.

8. What did Jesus wash for the disciples, at the Last Supper, as a sign of humility?

Show answer

A) Their feet (John 13:5). He poured water into a basin and began to wash his disciples' feet.

9. With what sign did Judas betray Jesus, showing the soldiers whom to arrest?

Show answer

C) A kiss (Matthew 26:48). Judas had given them a signal: "The one I kiss is the man; arrest him."

10. Who cut off the ear of the high priest's servant, during Jesus's arrest?

Show answer

D) Peter (Matthew 26:51). One of Jesus's companions... drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.

11. What crown did the soldiers place on Jesus, in mockery, before the crucifixion?

Show answer

C) A crown of thorns (Matthew 27:29). They twisted together a crown of thorns and set it on his head. They put a staff in his right hand.

12. What did Jesus say to the repentant criminal, crucified alongside him?

Show answer

A) "Today you will be with me in paradise" (Luke 23:43). Jesus answered him, "Truly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise."

13. What did Jesus cry out in a loud voice, just before dying, showing the suffering of separation?

Show answer

A) "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (Matthew 27:46). About the ninth hour Jesus cried out in a loud voice, "Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?" (which means "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?").

14. What did Jesus institute at the Last Supper, with the bread and wine?

Show answer

A) The Lord's Supper (Communion) (Matthew 26:26-28). Jesus took bread... and said, "Take and eat; this is my body," then the cup, saying, "This is my blood."

15. In which garden did Jesus pray, with a sorrowful heart, before his arrest?

Show answer

A) Gethsemane (Matthew 26:36). Then Jesus went with his disciples to a place called Gethsemane.

16. Who was released by Pilate instead of Jesus, at the crowd's request?

Show answer

B) Barabbas (Matthew 27:20-21,26). So he released Barabbas to them. But he had Jesus flogged, and handed him over to be crucified.

17. Who was the first person to see the risen Jesus, at the tomb?

Show answer

A) Mary Magdalene (John 20:14-16). Jesus said to her, "Mary." She turned toward him and cried out, "Rabboni!" (which means "Teacher").

18. What tore in two, at the moment of Jesus's death on the cross?

Show answer

A) The curtain of the Temple (Matthew 27:51). The curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom.

19. What tree did Jesus curse, because it had no fruit though it had leaves?

Show answer

C) A fig tree (Matthew 21:19). Then Jesus said to the tree, "May you never bear fruit again!" Immediately the tree withered.

20. What did Jesus observe about a poor widow, at the Temple treasury, praising the greatness of her gift?

Show answer

D) She gave two small coins, all she had to live on (Mark 12:43-44). This poor widow has put more into the treasury than all the others... she, out of her poverty, put in everything — all she had to live on.

21. What did Jesus tell his disciples, at the Last Supper, about one of them, without naming Judas directly at first?

Show answer

A) "One of you will betray me" (John 13:21). Jesus said, "Very truly I tell you, one of you is going to betray me."

22. Who fell asleep three times in the garden of Gethsemane, while Jesus prayed with a sorrowful heart?

Show answer

A) Peter, James and John (Matthew 26:40). Then he returned to his disciples and found them sleeping. "Couldn't you men keep watch with me for one hour?" he asked Peter.

23. What did all the disciples do, at Jesus's arrest in Gethsemane, to their great shame?

Show answer

D) They fled, abandoning him (Matthew 26:56). Then all the disciples deserted him and fled.

24. What did Peter do, right after the rooster crowed and Jesus looked at him?

Show answer

C) He went outside and wept bitterly (Luke 22:61-62). The Lord turned and looked straight at Peter... Peter went outside and wept bitterly.

25. What did Judas do, overcome with remorse, with the silver he received for betraying Jesus?

Show answer

B) He threw it into the Temple and hanged himself (Matthew 27:5). Judas threw the money into the temple and left. Then he went away and hanged himself.

26. What did the soldiers do with Jesus's undergarment, at the foot of the cross, so as not to tear it into pieces?

Show answer

A) They cast lots to see who would get it (John 19:23-24). "Let's not tear it," they said to one another. "Let's decide by lot who will get it." This happened that the scripture might be fulfilled.
