1. What was Paul's name before his conversion to Christianity?

Show answer

B) Saul (Acts 13:9). Saul, who was also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, began to preach.

2. On what road did Paul have the vision that converted him, temporarily blinding him?

Show answer

A) The road to Damascus (Acts 9:3-8). As he neared Damascus on his journey, suddenly a light from heaven flashed around him.

3. Who was Paul's traveling companion on his first missionary journey?

Show answer

A) Barnabas (Acts 13:2-3). The Holy Spirit said, "Set apart for me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them."

4. In what city was Paul imprisoned together with Silas, freed by an earthquake?

Show answer

D) Philippi (Acts 16:23-26). Suddenly there was such a violent earthquake that the foundations of the prison were shaken, and all the doors flew open.

5. How many major missionary journeys did Paul make, according to Acts?

Show answer

A) 3 (Acts 13-21). The Acts of the Apostles describe three great missionary journeys of Paul, in chapters 13-21.

6. What trade did Paul practice, learned in his youth and used during his travels?

Show answer

D) Tentmaker (Acts 18:3). Because he was a tentmaker as they were, he stayed and worked with them.

7. What citizenship did Paul hold, which helped him avoid an unjust flogging by Roman soldiers?

Show answer

A) Roman citizenship (Acts 22:25). Paul said to the centurion, "Is it legal for you to flog a Roman citizen who hasn't even been found guilty?"

8. What did Paul (Saul) hear, on the road to Damascus, asking him why he was persecuting him?

Show answer

C) "Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?" (Acts 9:4). He heard a voice saying to him, "Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?"

9. Who sang hymns together with Paul, in the prison at Philippi, right before the earthquake?

Show answer

A) Silas (Acts 16:25). About midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God.

10. What did the Philippian jailer ask, terrified, after the earthquake that freed Paul and Silas?

Show answer

B) "What must I do to be saved?" (Acts 16:29-30). He brought them out and asked, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"

11. Whom did Paul circumcise, because of the Jews of that region, though it was not a theological requirement?

Show answer

B) Timothy (Acts 16:3). Paul wanted to take him along... because of the Jews who lived in that area, he circumcised him.

12. Who was a skilled speaker in the Scriptures, taught more accurately by Priscilla and Aquila?

Show answer

C) Apollos (Acts 18:24-26). Apollos... was well versed in the Scriptures. Priscilla and Aquila... explained to him the way of God more adequately.

13. From what city did Paul originate, mentioned by himself as his birthplace?

Show answer

B) Tarsus, in Cilicia (Acts 22:3). I am a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia.

14. Under what famous teacher of the Law did Paul study, in his youth, in Jerusalem?

Show answer

D) Gamaliel (Acts 22:3). I was brought up in this city, at the feet of Gamaliel, thoroughly trained in the law of our ancestors.

15. Who was Paul's first European convert, a dealer in purple cloth from Thyatira?

Show answer

A) Lydia (Acts 16:14-15). A woman named Lydia, a dealer in purple cloth... was listening, and the Lord opened her heart.

16. What did Paul preach at the Areopagus in Athens, referring to an altar "To an unknown god"?

Show answer

A) The true God, the Creator (Acts 17:22-23). "What you worship as something unknown I am going to proclaim to you," Paul said about the true God.

17. What couple of craftsmen (tentmakers) helped Paul in Corinth?

Show answer

D) Priscilla and Aquila (Acts 18:2-3). He found a Jew named Aquila... and his wife Priscilla; because he was a tentmaker as they were, he stayed and worked with them.

18. What riot broke out in Ephesus, because of Paul's preaching against idols?

Show answer

D) The riot of the silversmiths who made images of Artemis (Diana) (Acts 19:24-28). A silversmith named Demetrius... stirred up the crowd, shouting, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!"

19. Who fell asleep during Paul's lengthy sermon and fell from a third-story window?

Show answer

A) Eutychus (Acts 20:9). A young man named Eutychus... was sinking into a deep sleep... he fell to the ground from the third story.

20. What vision did Paul have at Troas, which led him to cross into Macedonia with the Gospel?

Show answer

D) A Macedonian man, saying "Come over to Macedonia and help us" (Acts 16:9). During the night Paul had a vision of a man of Macedonia standing and begging him, "Come over to Macedonia and help us."

21. What vow did Paul take, shaving his head at Cenchreae, before setting sail for Syria?

Show answer

D) A Nazirite vow (Acts 18:18). Paul... sailed for Syria... having his hair cut off at Cenchreae because of a vow he had taken.

22. How many disciples in Ephesus had not heard of the Holy Spirit, knowing only John's baptism?

Show answer

B) Twelve (Acts 19:7). There were about twelve men in all.

23. What items of Paul's were taken to the sick, for healing, in Ephesus?

Show answer

A) Handkerchiefs and aprons (Acts 19:12). Even handkerchiefs and aprons that had touched him were taken to the sick, and their illnesses were cured.

24. Who were the sons of Sceva, Jewish exorcists, overpowered and injured by the evil spirit they tried to drive out?

Show answer

D) Seven sons of the priest Sceva (Acts 19:14-15). Seven sons of Sceva... the evil spirit answered them, "Jesus I know, and Paul I know about, but who are you?"

25. What did the crowd in Thessalonica shout about Paul and his companions, outraged by their preaching?

Show answer

A) "These men have turned the world upside down" (Acts 17:6). These men who have caused trouble all over the world have now come here.

26. What did Paul confess about himself, concerning a "thorn in the flesh," a suffering left to humble him?

Show answer

B) A thorn in the flesh, a messenger of Satan, to torment him (2 Corinthians 12:7). To keep me from becoming conceited... there was given me a thorn in my flesh, a messenger of Satan, to torment me.

27. To whom did Paul write a personal letter, interceding for a runaway slave named Onesimus?

Show answer

D) Philemon (The Epistle to Philemon). The Epistle to Philemon is a personal letter from Paul, interceding for the runaway slave Onesimus.
