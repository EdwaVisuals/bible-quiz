1. Who confronted 450 prophets of Baal, on Mount Carmel?

Show answer

C) Elijah (1 Kings 18:19-40). Elijah challenged the prophets of Baal to a contest on Mount Carmel, and the LORD answered with fire from heaven.

2. Who was swallowed by a great fish, after fleeing the LORD's command to go to Nineveh?

Show answer

C) Jonah (Jonah 1:17). The LORD provided a huge fish to swallow Jonah, into the depths of the sea.

3. Who was taken up to heaven in a chariot of fire, in a whirlwind?

Show answer

C) Elijah (2 Kings 2:11). Suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire appeared and separated the two of them, and Elijah went up to heaven in a whirlwind.

4. Who healed the leprosy of the Syrian general Naaman, commanding him to bathe seven times in the Jordan?

Show answer

C) Elisha (2 Kings 5:10-14). Elisha sent word to Naaman to wash seven times in the Jordan, and his flesh was restored and became clean.

5. Which prophet had a vision of dry bones coming to life?

Show answer

B) Ezekiel (Ezekiel 37:1-10). The LORD brought Ezekiel to a valley full of dry bones, which received flesh and life at the prophet's word.

6. Who was fed by ravens, by the brook Cherith, during a famine?

Show answer

C) Elijah (1 Kings 17:4-6). The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning and bread and meat in the evening, and he drank from the brook.

7. Which widow had flour and oil that never ran out, because of Elijah's miracle?

Show answer

C) The widow of Zarephath (1 Kings 17:14). Elijah said, "The jar of flour will not be used up and the jug of oil will not run dry, until the day the LORD sends rain."

8. What did Elijah do for the son of the widow of Zarephath, who had died?

Show answer

B) He raised him to life (1 Kings 17:21-22). Elijah stretched himself out on the boy three times and cried out to the LORD; the boy's life returned to him, and he lived.

9. Where did Elijah flee, a forty-day journey, out of fear of Jezebel, after the contest on Carmel?

Show answer

A) Mount Horeb (1 Kings 19:8). He got up and ate and drank... and traveled forty days and forty nights until he reached Horeb, the mountain of God.

10. In what way did the LORD appear to Elijah on Mount Horeb, not in a strong wind, earthquake, or fire?

Show answer

C) A gentle whisper (1 Kings 19:11-12). After the earthquake came a fire... and after the fire came a gentle whisper.

11. Whom did some boys mock, calling him "baldhead," and were then punished by two bears?

Show answer

C) Elisha (2 Kings 2:23-24). Some boys came out of the town and jeered at him, "Get out of here, baldy!"... two bears came out of the woods and mauled forty-two of the boys.

12. Who was Elisha's servant, punished with Naaman's leprosy for his greed?

Show answer

A) Gehazi (2 Kings 5:26-27). "Naaman's leprosy will cling to you and to your descendants forever." Gehazi went from Elisha's presence leprous.

13. What did Elijah pour three times over the sacrifice and wood, on Mount Carmel, before calling down fire from heaven?

Show answer

B) Water (1 Kings 18:33-34). Elijah said, "Fill four large jars with water and pour it on the offering and on the wood."... They did it a second and a third time.

14. To what pagan city was Jonah sent to warn, initially refusing the LORD's call?

Show answer

B) Nineveh (Jonah 1:2; 3:2). The LORD commanded Jonah to go to Nineveh, that great city, and preach against it.

15. Which prophet was called the "weeping prophet," mourning greatly over his people's fate?

Show answer

C) Jeremiah (Lamentations of Jeremiah). Jeremiah is traditionally called the "weeping prophet," and the book of Lamentations is attributed to him.

16. What did King Jehoiakim order done with the scroll written by Jeremiah, read before him?

Show answer

C) Burned in the fire (Jeremiah 36:23). As the scroll was read, the king cut off the columns with a scribe's knife and threw them into the fire, until the entire scroll was burned.

17. Which prophet married, at the LORD's command, an unfaithful woman, as a symbol of Israel's unfaithfulness?

Show answer

B) Hosea, to Gomer (Hosea 1:2-3). The LORD commanded Hosea to take a promiscuous woman, Gomer, as an image of Israel's broken covenant.

18. What prophecy did Micah make about the birthplace of the Messiah?

Show answer

A) Bethlehem (Micah 5:2). "But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah... out of you will come for me the one who will be ruler over Israel," Micah prophesied.

19. Whom did Elisha raise from the dead, at Shunem, with the LORD's help?

Show answer

C) The Shunammite woman's son (2 Kings 4:34-35). Elisha lay on top of the boy... the boy's body grew warm. He sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.

20. What did Elisha multiply for an indebted widow, so she could pay her debt and save her sons from slavery?

Show answer

B) Oil (2 Kings 4:6-7). The oil flowed until all the jars were full; go, sell the oil and pay your debts.

21. What did Elisha do for a pot of food that had become poisonous, at a gathering of prophets?

Show answer

D) He put flour in the pot and made it fit to eat (2 Kings 4:41). Elisha said, "Get some flour." He put it into the pot and said, "Serve it to the people to eat." And there was nothing harmful in the pot.

22. What vineyard did King Ahab desire, leading to the owner's murder through Jezebel's scheming?

Show answer

B) Naboth's vineyard (1 Kings 21:1-13). Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard... Ahab wanted it, and Jezebel schemed to have Naboth stoned to death on a false charge.

23. What happened to the Syrian army that came to capture Elisha at Dothan, through his prayer?

Show answer

A) It was struck with temporary blindness (2 Kings 6:18). Elisha prayed, "Strike this army, please, with blindness." So the LORD struck them with blindness, as Elisha had asked.

24. What miracle happened to a dead man, hastily thrown into a grave, touching Elisha's bones?

Show answer

A) He instantly came back to life (2 Kings 13:21). As soon as the man touched Elisha's bones, he came to life and stood up on his feet.

25. How long did the prophet Isaiah walk naked and barefoot, as a sign against Egypt and Cush?

Show answer

B) Three years (Isaiah 20:2-4). The LORD said, "Just as my servant Isaiah has gone stripped and barefoot for three years... so the king of Assyria will lead away the Egyptian captives."

26. What did Elijah say, mocking the prophets of Baal, who cried out without answer on Mount Carmel?

Show answer

C) That maybe their god was sleeping or away somewhere (1 Kings 18:27). Elijah began to taunt them: "Shout louder! Surely he is a god... Perhaps he is deep in thought, or busy, or traveling. Maybe he is sleeping and must be awakened."
