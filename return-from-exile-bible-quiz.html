1. Which Persian king issued a decree, allowing the Jews to return and rebuild the Temple?

Show answer

D) Cyrus (Ezra 1:1-4; 2 Chronicles 36:22-23). In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, the LORD moved his heart to release the people to return and build the Temple.

2. Who led the first group of Jews returning from exile, as governor, laying the Temple's foundation?

Show answer

A) Zerubbabel (Ezra 3:8-10). Zerubbabel and those who returned with him laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD.

3. Who led the rebuilding of the walls of Jerusalem, despite the opposition of enemies?

Show answer

B) Nehemiah (Nehemiah 2:17-18). Nehemiah called out to the residents, "Let us start rebuilding the wall of Jerusalem!", and the work began.

4. What position did Nehemiah hold at the Persian king's court, before going to Jerusalem?

Show answer

C) The king's cupbearer (Nehemiah 1:11; 2:1). Nehemiah was cupbearer to King Artaxerxes, a position of great trust at court.

5. Who publicly read the Law of Moses to the people returned from exile, in Jerusalem?

Show answer

C) Ezra (Nehemiah 8:1-3). Ezra the scribe brought the Law before the assembly and read it from daybreak till noon.

6. What did the elders who had seen the first Temple do, at the laying of the foundation of the new one?

Show answer

D) They wept loudly, remembering the glory of the old one (Ezra 3:12). Many of the priests, Levites and heads of families... wept aloud when they saw the foundation of this temple.

7. From what priestly family did Ezra, the scribe who brought the Law to Jerusalem, descend?

Show answer

D) From the family of Aaron, the high priest (Ezra 7:1-5). Ezra was the son of Seraiah... son of Aaron the chief priest.

8. What did Ezra refuse to ask King Artaxerxes for, before the journey to Jerusalem, trusting in God?

Show answer

A) A guard of soldiers and horsemen for the road (Ezra 8:21-22). I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us... for we had told the king, "The gracious hand of our God is on everyone who looks to him."

9. How did Nehemiah inspect the ruined walls of Jerusalem, the first night, before revealing his plan?

Show answer

D) Riding a donkey, at night (Nehemiah 2:12). I went out at night... there were no mounts with me except the one I was riding on.

10. What did Nehemiah refuse to take from the people, unlike the governors before him?

Show answer

C) The food and tax due to a governor (Nehemiah 5:15). The earlier governors... had placed a heavy burden on the people... but out of reverence for God I did not act like that.

11. Who was the last prophet of the Old Testament, whose writings close the prophetic canon?

Show answer

A) Malachi (The Book of Malachi). The book of Malachi is the last prophetic book of the Old Testament, in the order of the Christian canon.

12. About how many servants and maids, besides the nearly 42,360 free people, came with the first wave of returned exiles?

Show answer

C) About 7,337 (Ezra 2:65). Besides these, they had 7,337 male and female servants.

13. What did the Jews build first, even before laying the foundation of the new Temple, so they could offer sacrifices?

Show answer

C) The altar, on its former foundation (Ezra 3:2). They built the altar of the God of Israel to sacrifice burnt offerings on it, in accordance with what is written in the Law of Moses.

14. In how many days was the wall of Jerusalem finished, under Nehemiah's leadership?

Show answer

A) 52 days (Nehemiah 6:15). The wall was completed on the twenty-fifth of Elul, in fifty-two days.

15. Who were the two main enemies who tried to stop the rebuilding of the walls of Jerusalem?

Show answer

A) Sanballat and Tobiah (Nehemiah 2:10,19). Sanballat and Tobiah became greatly angered when they heard that work was going on to repair the wall of Jerusalem.

16. Which two prophets encouraged the Jews to finish rebuilding the Temple, halted for years?

Show answer

C) Haggai and Zechariah (Ezra 5:1-2). Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem, urging them to resume the work.

17. How did Nehemiah learn about the state of Jerusalem's walls, which caused him to weep and fast?

Show answer

A) Through his brother Hanani and other men who came from Judah (Nehemiah 1:2-4). Hanani, one of my brothers, came from Judah... and told Nehemiah about Jerusalem's broken-down walls.

18. What did the wall workers do, because of the enemies' threats, holding a weapon in one hand?

Show answer

A) They built with one hand and held a weapon with the other (Nehemiah 4:17). Those who built the wall... worked with one hand and held a weapon in the other.

19. About how many Jews returned from exile, on the first journey led by Zerubbabel?

Show answer

A) About 42,360 (Ezra 2:64). The whole company numbered 42,360.

20. How many sacred Temple vessels did Cyrus order returned to the Jews, at their return from exile?

Show answer

C) 5,400 (Ezra 1:11). In all, there were 5,400 articles of gold and of silver.

21. What did King Darius order, after searching the archives and confirming Cyrus's decree?

Show answer

B) The Temple work was to continue, funded from the king's treasury (Ezra 6:7-8). The officials of Trans-Euphrates... are to stay away from there... the expenses are to be fully paid to these men out of the royal treasury.

22. What serious problem did Ezra find upon arriving in Jerusalem, related to the people's marriages?

Show answer

A) They had married women from pagan nations, breaking the Law (Ezra 9:1-2). The people of Israel... have not kept themselves separate from the neighboring peoples... they have taken some of their daughters as wives.

23. How did Nehemiah organize the wall builders, so that the work would go quickly?

Show answer

C) Each family worked on the section opposite their own house (Nehemiah 3:28). Each of them repaired the section opposite their own house.

24. What did Nehemiah answer, several times, when Sanballat summoned him to a suspicious meeting, far from the work?

Show answer

B) "I am doing a great work and I cannot come down" (Nehemiah 6:3). I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am carrying on a great project and cannot go down."

25. Who was hired by the enemies to frighten Nehemiah, pretending to be a prophet and advising him to hide in the Temple?

Show answer

B) Shemaiah (Nehemiah 6:10-13). Shemaiah... wanted to intimidate me so that I would sin by doing what he suggested.

26. What did Nehemiah appoint at the gates of Jerusalem, after finishing the wall, for order and security?

Show answer

A) Gatekeepers, singers and Levites with fixed duties (Nehemiah 7:1). I gave the city gates: gatekeepers, singers and Levites were appointed.
