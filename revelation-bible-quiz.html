1. Who received the visions of Revelation, exiled on the island of Patmos?

Show answer

D) The apostle John (Revelation 1:1,9). I, John, your brother... was on the island of Patmos because of the word of God.

2. How many churches receive direct messages from Christ, at the beginning of Revelation?

Show answer

D) 7 (Revelation 1:4,11). Grace and peace to you from him who is, and who was, and who is to come... to the seven churches in the province of Asia.

3. Who is described as "the Lamb, who looked as if it had been slain," the only one worthy to open the scroll?

Show answer

B) Jesus Christ (Revelation 5:6-9). I saw... a Lamb, looking as if it had been slain... You are worthy... because you were slain.

4. What number is given as "the mark of the beast" in Revelation?

Show answer

C) 666 (Revelation 13:18). This calls for wisdom. Let the person who has insight calculate the number of the beast... that number is 666.

5. What new city comes down from heaven, at the end of Revelation, as the eternal dwelling of God with people?

Show answer

B) The New Jerusalem (Revelation 21:2). I saw the Holy City, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God.

6. What did a voice like a trumpet command John, at the beginning of his vision?

Show answer

A) To write on a scroll what he sees (Revelation 1:10-11). I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet, which said: "Write on a scroll what you see."

7. How was the hair of "one like a son of man" described, in John's first vision?

Show answer

C) White like wool, as white as snow (Revelation 1:14). The hair on his head was white like wool, as white as snow.

8. What did "one like a son of man" hold in his right hand, in John's vision?

Show answer

C) Seven stars (Revelation 1:16). In his right hand he held seven stars.

9. What was the church of Ephesus reproached for, as its main fault, by Christ?

Show answer

C) It had forsaken its first love (Revelation 2:4). Yet I hold this against you: You have forsaken the love you had at first.

10. What was promised to the church of Smyrna, faithful even unto death?

Show answer

B) The crown of life (Revelation 2:10). Be faithful, even to the point of death, and I will give you life as your victor's crown.

11. What door was promised to the church of Philadelphia, one that no one can shut?

Show answer

D) An open door (Revelation 3:8). See, I have placed before you an open door that no one can shut.

12. What do the seven golden lampstands represent, among which "one like a son of man" walked?

Show answer

C) The seven churches (Revelation 1:20). The seven lampstands you saw are the seven churches.

13. Whom did John see walking among the seven golden lampstands, in his first vision?

Show answer

D) Someone like a son of man (Revelation 1:13). And among the lampstands was someone like a son of man.

14. How many elders surround the throne of God, in the vision of Revelation 4?

Show answer

D) 24 (Revelation 4:4). Surrounding the throne were twenty-four other thrones, and seated on them were twenty-four elders.

15. How many apocalyptic horsemen are released at the opening of the first four seals?

Show answer

A) 4 (Revelation 6:1-8). The Lamb opened the first... the second... the third... the fourth seal, and out came four horses and their riders.

16. How many people are sealed, "from all the tribes of Israel," in Revelation 7?

Show answer

B) 144,000 (Revelation 7:4). I heard the number of those who were sealed: 144,000.

17. Who is the angel who fights and defeats the dragon in the war in heaven?

Show answer

B) Michael (Revelation 12:7-9). Then war broke out in heaven. Michael and his angels fought against the dragon.

18. What is the sinful city that falls, a symbol of the world's corruption, in Revelation 18, called?

Show answer

B) Babylon the Great (Revelation 18:2). "Fallen! Fallen is Babylon the Great!"

19. How many heads and horns did the great red dragon have, in John's vision?

Show answer

D) Seven heads and ten horns (Revelation 12:3). Then another sign appeared in heaven: an enormous red dragon with seven heads and ten horns.

20. For how many days did the woman clothed with the sun flee into the wilderness, fed there by God?

Show answer

A) 1,260 days (Revelation 12:6). The woman fled into the wilderness to a place prepared for her by God, where she might be taken care of for 1,260 days.

21. Where was the dragon thrown, after losing the battle against Michael and his angels?

Show answer

C) Down to the earth (Revelation 12:9). The great dragon... was hurled to the earth, and his angels with him.

22. What mandatory mark did the beast require, so that the small and great could buy or sell?

Show answer

C) A mark on the right hand or forehead (Revelation 13:16-17). It also forced all people... to receive a mark on their right hands or on their foreheads, so that they could not buy or sell unless they had the mark.

23. What happened to the two witnesses of Revelation 11, after they were killed, to the world's amazement?

Show answer

B) They rose to life after three and a half days, ascending to heaven (Revelation 11:11-12). After three and a half days the breath of life from God entered them... they went up to heaven in a cloud.

24. What happens at the opening of the seventh seal, just before the sound of the trumpets?

Show answer

C) There is silence in heaven, for about half an hour (Revelation 8:1). When he opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.

25. How many trumpets are sounded by angels, bringing successive plagues on the earth?

Show answer

B) Seven (Revelation 8:6). The seven angels who had the seven trumpets prepared to sound them.
