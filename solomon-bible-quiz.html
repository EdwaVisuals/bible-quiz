1. What did Solomon ask God for, in a dream at Gibeon, instead of riches or a long life?

Show answer

B) Wisdom, to judge the people (1 Kings 3:5-9). Solomon asked for a discerning heart, to judge the people and to distinguish between good and evil.

2. Who was King Solomon's father?

Show answer

D) David (2 Samuel 12:24). Solomon was the son of David and Bathsheba, their second child.

3. What important building did Solomon construct in Jerusalem, fulfilling his father David's wish?

Show answer

A) The LORD's Temple (1 Kings 6:1,14). Solomon built the LORD's Temple in Jerusalem, a work his father David had longed for but was not allowed to do.

4. What queen came from afar to visit Solomon, amazed by his wisdom and wealth?

Show answer

D) The Queen of Sheba (1 Kings 10:1). The Queen of Sheba heard of Solomon's fame and came to test him with hard questions.

5. How many years did it take for Solomon's Temple to be finished?

Show answer

A) 7 years (1 Kings 6:38). The Temple was built over seven years, until it was finished in every detail.

6. At what city did the dream take place, in which Solomon asked God for wisdom?

Show answer

B) Gibeon (1 Kings 3:4-5). The king went to Gibeon to offer sacrifices there, for that was the most important high place.

7. What else did God promise Solomon, besides wisdom, because he had not asked for it?

Show answer

D) Riches and honor (1 Kings 3:12-13). I will give you a wise and discerning heart... I will also give you what you have not asked for: riches and honor.

8. Whom did Solomon marry, as a political alliance, at the beginning of his reign?

Show answer

C) Pharaoh's daughter (1 Kings 3:1). Solomon made an alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt and married his daughter.

9. How many years did it take to build Solomon's own palace, longer than the Temple?

Show answer

A) 13 years (1 Kings 7:1). It took Solomon thirteen years, however, to complete the construction of his palace.

10. How many proverbs is King Solomon said to have spoken, testimony to his wisdom?

Show answer

C) Three thousand (1 Kings 4:32). He spoke three thousand proverbs and his songs numbered a thousand and five.

11. From what country did Solomon import horses, for his chariots and horsemen?

Show answer

D) Egypt (1 Kings 10:28). Solomon's horses were imported from Egypt.

12. How many years did Solomon reign over all Israel, at Jerusalem, in total?

Show answer

A) 40 years (1 Kings 11:42). Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years.

13. With what precious wood, brought from Lebanon, did Solomon line the interior of the Temple?

Show answer

A) Cedar wood (1 Kings 6:15-18). Solomon lined the interior walls of the Temple with cedar boards... no stone was to be seen anywhere.

14. How did Solomon settle the dispute between two women who both claimed to be the mother of the same living child?

Show answer

A) He ordered the child cut in two, to find the true mother (1 Kings 3:24-27). Solomon ordered the baby cut in two — and the true mother, horrified, was willing to give up the child, to save its life.

15. What king of Tyre helped Solomon with cedar wood and craftsmen, for building the Temple?

Show answer

A) Hiram (1 Kings 5:8-10). Hiram, king of Tyre, sent Solomon cedar and cypress wood, along with skilled craftsmen.

16. What was brought into the Most Holy Place of the new Temple, at its dedication?

Show answer

D) The ark of the covenant (1 Kings 8:6). The priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, in the Most Holy Place of the Temple.

17. About how many horsemen and chariots did Solomon have, according to the account of his military power?

Show answer

C) About 12,000 horsemen (1 Kings 4:26). Solomon had four thousand stalls for horses and chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.

18. What books of the Bible are largely attributed to Solomon?

Show answer

B) Proverbs, Ecclesiastes and Song of Songs (Proverbs 1:1; Ecclesiastes 1:1; Song of Songs 1:1). Solomon, known for his wisdom, is considered the principal author of Proverbs, Ecclesiastes and Song of Songs.

19. What were the two bronze pillars at the entrance of Solomon's Temple named?

Show answer

A) Jachin and Boaz (1 Kings 7:21). He erected the pillars at the portico of the temple... he named the one on the right Jachin and the one on the left Boaz.

20. What large bronze object, for washing the priests, stood on the backs of twelve bronze oxen, in the Temple courtyard?

Show answer

C) The Sea (the bronze Sea) (1 Kings 7:23-25). He made the Sea... it stood on twelve bulls, three facing north, three facing west.

21. What sign of God's presence filled the Temple, at its dedication by Solomon?

Show answer

C) The cloud of the LORD's glory (1 Kings 8:10-11). The cloud filled the temple of the LORD... the glory of the LORD filled the temple of God.

22. From where did Solomon bring large quantities of gold, by ships sent to sea?

Show answer

D) From Ophir (1 Kings 9:28). Hiram's ships... brought back four hundred and twenty talents of gold from Ophir, and delivered it to King Solomon.

23. How many chief officials did Solomon have over the workers employed on his construction projects?

Show answer

A) 550 (1 Kings 9:23). They were the chief officials in charge of Solomon's projects: five hundred fifty.

24. What important cities did Solomon build or strengthen, besides Jerusalem?

Show answer

D) Hazor, Megiddo and Gezer (1 Kings 9:15). Here is the account of the forced labor King Solomon conscripted... to build Hazor, Megiddo and Gezer.

25. What metal became so common in Jerusalem, in Solomon's time, that it was considered as common as stones?

Show answer

D) Silver (1 Kings 10:27). The king made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones.

26. For how many days did Solomon hold the feast of the Temple's dedication, with all Israel?

Show answer

C) Seven days, followed by another seven (1 Kings 8:65). So Solomon observed the festival at that time... for seven days, and all Israel with him... then for seven more days.
