1. God created the world in six days and rested on the seventh.

Show answer

True (Genesis 1-2). The Lord worked six days at creation, and on the seventh he rested.

2. Noah built the ark out of oak wood.

Show answer

False (Genesis 6:14). Noah was commanded to build the ark out of gopher wood, not oak.

3. Abraham was ready to sacrifice his son Isaac, at God's command.

Show answer

True (Genesis 22). God tested Abraham, asking him to offer Isaac as a sacrifice.

4. Joseph was sold by his brothers as a slave in Babylon.

Show answer

False (Genesis 37:28). Joseph was sold and taken to Egypt, not Babylon.

5. Moses parted the Red Sea, so the Israelites could cross on dry ground.

Show answer

True (Exodus 14). Moses stretched out his hand, and the waters of the Red Sea parted.

6. The Ten Commandments were given on Mount Carmel.

Show answer

False (Exodus 20). The Ten Commandments were given on Mount Sinai, not Carmel.

7. Joshua conquered Jericho after the Israelites marched around the city for seven days.

Show answer

True (Joshua 6). At the sound of the trumpets, on the seventh day, Jericho's walls fell down.

8. Samson lost his strength when his beard was cut off.

Show answer

False (Judges 16). Samson's strength was in his uncut hair, not his beard.

9. David defeated Goliath with a sling and a stone.

Show answer

True (1 Samuel 17). David brought down the giant Goliath with a single stone, hurled from a sling.

10. Solomon built the Temple in three years.

Show answer

False (1 Kings 6:38). Solomon's Temple was built over seven years, not three.

11. Elijah was taken up to heaven in a chariot of fire.

Show answer

True (2 Kings 2:11). A chariot of fire and horses of fire separated Elijah from Elisha, and Elijah went up to heaven.

12. Daniel was thrown into a fiery furnace.

Show answer

False (Daniel 3, 6). Daniel was thrown into the lions' den; his friends were the ones thrown into the fiery furnace.

13. Jonah was swallowed by a great fish, after fleeing the Lord's command.

Show answer

True (Jonah 1:17). The Lord appointed a great fish, which swallowed Jonah.

14. Esther was the queen of Egypt.

Show answer

False (Esther 2:17). Esther was the queen of Persia, wife of King Ahasuerus, not of Egypt.

15. Nehemiah rebuilt Jerusalem's walls in 52 days.

Show answer

True (Nehemiah 6:15). The wall was finished in fifty-two days, under Nehemiah's leadership.

16. Jesus was born in Nazareth.

Show answer

False (Luke 2:4-7). Jesus was born in Bethlehem; he grew up in Nazareth.

17. The magi brought Jesus gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

Show answer

True (Matthew 2:11). The magi from the east brought the child gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

18. Jesus turned water into wine at a wedding in Jerusalem.

Show answer

False (John 2:1-11). Jesus' first miracle took place at the wedding in Cana, not Jerusalem.

19. Jesus fed 5,000 people with five loaves and two fish.

Show answer

True (Matthew 14:17-21). Jesus multiplied the five loaves and two fish, feeding a crowd of nearly 5,000 men.

20. Peter denied Jesus seven times.

Show answer

False (Matthew 26:34,75). Peter denied Jesus three times, not seven.

21. Jesus was crucified on the hill of Golgotha.

Show answer

True (Matthew 27:33). Jesus was taken to Golgotha, "the Place of the Skull", and crucified there.

22. Thomas believed in Jesus' resurrection immediately, without any proof.

Show answer

False (John 20:24-27). Thomas doubted, until he saw and touched Jesus' wounds.

23. The Holy Spirit came down on the apostles on the Day of Pentecost.

Show answer

True (Acts 2:1-4). A mighty wind and tongues like fire accompanied the descent of the Holy Spirit.

24. Stephen was the first apostle chosen by Jesus.

Show answer

False (Acts 7:59-60). Stephen was the first Christian martyr, not one of the twelve apostles.

25. Saul was blinded by a light from heaven, on the road to Damascus.

Show answer

True (Acts 9:3-8). A powerful light from heaven temporarily blinded Saul, the future apostle Paul.

26. Paul was shipwrecked near the island of Cyprus, on his way to Rome.

Show answer

False (Acts 27-28). Paul's shipwreck happened near the island of Malta, not Cyprus.

27. John received the visions of Revelation while exiled on the island of Patmos.

Show answer

True (Revelation 1:9). John was in exile on the island of Patmos when he received the visions of Revelation.

28. The number of the beast in Revelation is 777.

Show answer

False (Revelation 13:18). The number of the beast is 666, not 777.

29. Cain killed his brother Abel, out of envy.

Show answer

True (Genesis 4:8). Cain grew angry because Abel's offering was accepted and his was not, and he killed him.

30. The Tower of Babel was built so people could reach Egypt.

Show answer

False (Genesis 11:4). The Tower of Babel was built so people could reach heaven, not Egypt.

31. Ruth was a Moabite woman, David's ancestor.

Show answer

True (Ruth 1, 4). Ruth, the Moabite, became, through Boaz, David's great-grandmother.

32. Gideon fought with 3,000 men against the Midianites.

Show answer

False (Judges 7:2-7). The Lord reduced Gideon's army to just 300 men.

33. Saul was the first king of Israel, anointed by Samuel.

Show answer

True (1 Samuel 10:1). Samuel anointed Saul as Israel's first king.

34. Absalom, David's son, drowned in a river.

Show answer

False (2 Samuel 18:9-14). Absalom died caught by his hair in an oak tree, run through by Joab.

35. The Queen of Sheba visited Solomon, amazed by his wisdom.

Show answer

True (1 Kings 10). The Queen of Sheba came to test Solomon with hard questions and was left amazed.

36. Ezekiel had a vision of precious stones coming to life.

Show answer

False (Ezekiel 37:1-10). Ezekiel's vision was of dry bones coming to life, not precious stones.

37. Zacchaeus climbed a tree to see Jesus.

Show answer

True (Luke 19:2-4). Zacchaeus, short in stature, climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus.

38. Lazarus was raised by Jesus after seven days in the tomb.

Show answer

False (John 11:39-44). Lazarus had been dead four days, not seven, when Jesus raised him.

39. Hannah prayed fervently to God for a son, and bore Samuel.

Show answer

True (1 Samuel 1). Hannah prayed in deep anguish of soul for a son, and the Lord gave her Samuel.

40. Joseph became ruler over all Egypt at the age of 50.

Show answer

False (Genesis 41:46). Joseph was thirty years old when he became ruler, not fifty.

41. God created Adam from the dust of the ground.

Show answer

True (Genesis 2:7). The Lord God formed man from the dust of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life.

42. Eve was created from Adam's rib.

Show answer

True (Genesis 2:22). The Lord God made a woman from the rib he had taken from Adam.

43. Noah took one pair of every animal, clean and unclean alike.

Show answer

False (Genesis 7:2). Of the clean animals he took seven pairs; of the unclean, only one pair.

44. The rainbow was the sign of God's covenant with Noah, that he would never again send a flood.

Show answer

True (Genesis 9:13). The rainbow in the clouds was given as the sign of the covenant between God and the earth.

45. Lot's wife looked back toward Sodom and became a pillar of salt.

Show answer

True (Genesis 19:26). Disregarding the command not to look back, Lot's wife turned into a pillar of salt.

46. Jacob wrestled with an angel all night, and his name was changed to Israel.

Show answer

True (Genesis 32:24-28). After the struggle, the angel told him: "Your name shall no longer be Jacob, but Israel."

47. Abraham was 100 years old when Isaac was born.

Show answer

True (Genesis 21:5). Abraham was a hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.

48. Sarah laughed when she heard she would have a son in her old age.

Show answer

True (Genesis 18:12). Sarah laughed to herself, not believing she could have a child at her age.

49. Abraham sent his servant to find a wife for Isaac, and he found Rebekah.

Show answer

True (Genesis 24). Abraham's servant found Rebekah at the well, exactly as he had prayed to God.

50. Joseph's colorful coat was a gift from Pharaoh.

Show answer

False (Genesis 37:3). The colorful coat was a gift from his father, Jacob, who loved him more than his brothers.

51. Joseph's brothers first threw him into a pit, before selling him to traders.

Show answer

True (Genesis 37:24). They threw him into an empty pit, then sold him to Ishmaelite traders.

52. Joseph interpreted the dreams of Pharaoh's cupbearer and baker, in prison.

Show answer

True (Genesis 40). While imprisoned, Joseph correctly interpreted the dreams of Pharaoh's two servants.

53. Egypt had seven years of plenty, followed by seven years of famine, after Pharaoh's dream.

Show answer

True (Genesis 41). Joseph interpreted Pharaoh's dream: seven years of plenty, then seven years of famine.

54. Moses was found in a basket on the Nile river by Pharaoh's daughter.

Show answer

True (Exodus 2:5-6). Pharaoh's daughter found the basket floating on the Nile and raised Moses as her own son.

55. God appeared to Moses in a bush that was burning but not consumed.

Show answer

True (Exodus 3:2). The angel of the Lord appeared to Moses in a flame, in the middle of a bush that burned without being consumed.

56. Aaron's staff turned into a dove before Pharaoh.

Show answer

False (Exodus 7:10). Aaron's staff turned into a snake, not a dove.

57. The last plague of Egypt was the death of the firstborn.

Show answer

True (Exodus 12:29). The tenth plague, the harshest, was the death of every firstborn in Egypt.

58. The Israelites ate manna in the wilderness for forty years.

Show answer

True (Exodus 16:35). The children of Israel ate manna until they reached the land of Canaan, for forty years.

59. The Israelites built a golden calf while Moses was on Mount Sinai.

Show answer

True (Exodus 32). Losing their patience, the people asked Aaron for a golden calf, to worship.

60. All twelve spies sent into Canaan brought back an encouraging report.

Show answer

False (Numbers 13-14). Only Joshua and Caleb encouraged the people; the other ten brought back a discouraging report.
